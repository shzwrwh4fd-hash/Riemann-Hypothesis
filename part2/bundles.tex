\subsection{Introduction}\label{pf:bundles:intro}

This section proves \cref{thm:member-bundles} and \cref{prop:families:mass}. The theorem has three conclusions:
the Siegel mass ratio for standard hermitian lattices, the member-existence statement $\sfname{MemberE}$ and the
bundle-existence statement $\sfname{ExBL}$ for the parameter $\bundp_A$ obtained from the origin extension by adjoining an
at-property $A$. All objects are records of the formal development \cite{LeanSource2026}; \cref{pf:bundles:framework}
recalls them, unfolded as far as the proofs of this section need, and introduces short notation for them.

\paragraph{Structure of the proof.}
The proof is an assembly. From the first bundle $P$ of classical results of \cref{sec:families:theta} and from
$\sfname{RowRat}$ one forms three \emph{hypothesis packages} (\cref{pf:bundles:packages}); one component of the first
package is the mass ratio, which is a theorem (\cref{pf:bundles:mass}). Four inputs are then supplied to a member-existence
theorem proved elsewhere (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.exists_baseLattice_of_prime_alt}):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the packages;
\item the existence, eventually and for large twisting data, of \emph{unlocalised saturated states} at every starting
column witness (\cref{pf:bundles:saturated});
\item the \emph{multiplicity condition}: equality of cuspidal multiplicities of the definite group $\Uni(Y_0)$ and of the
groups $\Uni(W_i)$ at every weighted point (\cref{pf:bundles:multcond});
\item the elliptic and local Langlands packages, obtained elsewhere from two components of $P$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.hyps_e_third_layer_ser_n_of_geometric_galois}).
\end{enumerate}
The member-existence statement so obtained concerns the origin extension; it is transported to the extension by the
at-property $A$ by a logical argument (\cref{pf:bundles:atprop}). The bundle statement is proved directly for the enriched
parameter (\cref{pf:bundles:exbl}): at every good point a bundle of every size is assembled from a reciprocity record
compatible with the weighted point, the zeta constants of the point and a selected realisation.

\paragraph{Mathematical content.}
Most of the part of the formal proof treated here consists of constructions of large records (stages, pre-data, states,
levels) and of transport lemmas between them. Its analytic core is a \emph{Rallis inner product formula along twisted
theta chains} (\cref{pf:bundles:chains}): along the chain of theta lifts $\Uni(Y_0)\to\Uni(Y_1)\to\dots\to\Uni(X')$ between
definite unitary groups, the Petersson norm of the lift of an eigenform equals its norm times a positive constant times a
partial twisted standard $L$-value at the point $(1+t)/2$, the constants being independent of the eigenform and of the
twisting characters. It enters the member construction through the lower unitary case of the construction of states.

\paragraph{Organisation.}
\Cref{pf:bundles:framework} recalls the framework (eventual quantifiers, twisting data, cores and slots, member
existence, bundles). \Cref{pf:bundles:mass} proves the mass ratio, \cref{pf:bundles:packages} builds the hypothesis
packages, \cref{pf:bundles:transfer} proves the transfer lemmas (at-properties, movement of positions, multiplicity
condition), \cref{pf:bundles:exbl} the existence of linked bundles and \cref{pf:bundles:saturated} the existence of
unlocalised saturated states. \Cref{pf:bundles:chains} treats the inner product formula along theta chains, and
\cref{pf:bundles:proofmain} assembles the proof of \cref{thm:member-bundles}.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The part of the formal proof treated here comprises about $106\,000$ lines besides the member-existence theorem cited above.
We give complete proofs of the assembly, of the transfer lemmas, of the reductions of the bundle statement and
of the existence of saturated states to their inputs, and of the inner product formula along theta chains from its step
statements. We also prove the principal constructions used as inputs: fine real data and their extension to larger
coefficient fields, the weight case, saturation and starting choices (\cref{pf:bundles:saturated}), the computations at the
twisted places of the theta chains (\cref{pf:bundles:twisted}), and columns, spectral data and realisations at weighted points
(\cref{pf:bundles:exbl}). The deeper constructions on which these rest are cited with their statements.

\subsection{The membership framework}\label{pf:bundles:framework}

The data types of this part of the development are records with many components. We name the components that the proofs
below manipulate and describe the others by their role; the logical structure (order of quantifiers, thresholds,
exceptional sets) is reproduced exactly.

\paragraph{Scale contexts and eventual quantifiers.}
A \emph{scale context} $\bundK$ consists of a scale $s=(x,\eps,J)$ with $x>1$, $0<\eps<1/52$, $J\ge1$, a standard record $C$
of family constants, two reals $c_D,c_h$, a pre-character datum (ranks, host, words and level primes), integer data, a sign
pattern, a level datum and compatibility conditions between them; we write $x(\bundK)$ and $\eps(\bundK)$. A pre-base (see
below) \emph{lies under} $\bundK$ if it is obtained from the pre-character datum and the level datum of $\bundK$ for some
choice of hermitian spaces with diagonal Gram matrices. For reals $C_5,c_6,C_b$ the context is \emph{$(C_5,c_6,C_b)$-good} if
a fixed list of inequalities between its numerical data holds.

\begin{definition}[Eventual quantifiers]\label{pf:bundles:def:ev}
Let $p$ be a predicate on scale contexts.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\mathrm{Ev}(p)$ (``$p$ holds eventually'') means: there is $\eps_0>0$ such that for all $\eps$ with $0<\eps\le\eps_0$,
$\eps<1/52$, and all $C_8\ge1$ there is a transported threshold $K_0$ such that for every standard constant record $C$ whose
constant $C_{\mathrm{pde}}$ equals $C_8$ and which is large with respect to $K_0$ there is $c_1$ such that for all $c_D\ge c_1$
there is $c_2$ such that for all $c_h\ge c_2$ there is $x_0$ with $p(\bundK)$ for all contexts $\bundK$ with constants
$(C,c_D,c_h)$, $\eps(\bundK)=\eps$ and $x(\bundK)\ge x_0$.
\item $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g}(p)$ is defined in the same way, with three further reals $C_5,c_6,C_b$ quantified universally
together with $C_8$ and with the further premise that $\bundK$ is $(C_5,c_6,C_b)$-good.
\item For a predicate $p(\kappa,\bundK)$ on parameter tuples $\kappa=(\eps,C,c_D,c_h,C_5,c_6,C_b)$ and contexts,
$\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}(p)$ has the quantifier prefix of $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g}$ up to $c_h$, followed by: if
$C_5,c_6,C_b>0$, there is $x_0$ such that $p(\kappa,\bundK)$ holds for all $(C_5,c_6,C_b)$-good contexts $\bundK$ with
constants $(C,c_D,c_h)$, exponent $\eps$ and $x(\bundK)\ge x_0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Bases, frames and twisting data.}
A \emph{pre-base} fixes the ranks, the host (a CM extension $E/F$ with its auxiliary primes), hermitian spaces and the words
with their Harish-Chandra parameters and level primes. A \emph{base} $B$ over a pre-base adds a coefficient field
$\mathit{LL}$, Galois over $\Q$, with an embedding into $\C$ and subfields $K_s\subseteq\mathit{LL}_0\subseteq\mathit{LL}$
subject to richness conditions; we write $\mathit{LL}_\C$ and $\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}$ for the images in $\C$ of $\mathit{LL}$ and
of the fixed coefficient field. A \emph{frame} $f$ is a Hecke-character frame of the host (characters
$\mu_0,\eta,\chi_\Psi,\delta,\delta'$ of $E$ and auxiliary data). A base $B'$ \emph{enlarges} $B$, written $B\preceq B'$, if
both lie over the same pre-base, the images of $K_s$ and of $\mathit{LL}_0$ in $\C$ agree, and
$\mathit{LL}_\C\subseteq\mathit{LL}'_\C$.

Twisting data are those of \cref{def:families:twist}: a twisting datum $T$ over $B$ consists of a finite set
$T.\mathrm{pl}$ of rational primes and, for each finite place $w$ of $\mathit{LL}$ with residue characteristic $\ell_w$, a
finite set $Q_w$ of primes of $\OO_E$ not above $\ell_w$, an exponent $k_w\in\N$ and a Hecke character $\psi_w$ of $E$ of
order dividing $\ell_w^{k_w}$, which is a twist character for the host and is related to $(\ell_w,Q_w)$ by a fixed
condition; $\psi_w=1$ and $k_w=0$ if $\ell_w\notin T.\mathrm{pl}$, and $\psi_w,k_w$ are invariant under conjugation of places.
The datum is \emph{untwisted} if $Q_w=\emptyset$, $k_w=0$ and $\psi_w=1$ for all $w$; we write $T^\circ$ for the untwisted
datum with the prime set of $T$. For a function $\mathcal A$ from $\N$ to finite subsets of $\N$, $T$ \emph{avoids}
$\mathcal A$ if no $v\in Q_w$ has absolute norm in $\mathcal A(\ell_w)$. For a frame $f$ let $\Xi(T,f)=\{1,\delta,\delta'\}\cup\{$the
twisted characters of $(T,f)\}$. Finally $T$ is \emph{forced} for $(\bundK,B)$ if every prime which is one of the auxiliary
primes $g_0,g_1,d$ of the host or is at most the size $X''(n)$ of the scale of $\bundK$ lies in $T.\mathrm{pl}$.

\begin{definition}[Large twisting data]\label{pf:bundles:def:large}
Let $\bundK$ be a context and $p(B,f,T)$ a predicate on bases, frames and twisting data. $\mathrm{Lg}_\bundK(p)$ (``$p$
holds for large twisting data at $\bundK$'') means: there are functions $S_1,S_2$ with values finite sets of algebraic
numbers, $P_1$ with values finite sets of rational primes and $\mathcal A$ with values functions from $\N$ to finite subsets
of $\N$, depending on the pre-base, the frame and the relevant subfields of $\C$ (and, for $S_2$, on a set of characters),
such that $p(B,f,T)$ holds for every base $B$ whose pre-base lies under $\bundK$, every frame $f$ and every twisting datum $T$
with $S_1\subseteq\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}$ and $S_2(\Xi(T,f))\subseteq\mathit{LL}_\C$, $P_1\subseteq T.\mathrm{pl}$, and $T$
avoiding $\mathcal A$. Put
\[
\sfname{EvLgG}(p)=\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g}\bigl(\bundK\mapsto\mathrm{Lg}_\bundK(p_\bundK)\bigr),\qquad
\mathrm{Ev}\,\mathrm{Lg}(p)=\mathrm{Ev}\bigl(\bundK\mapsto\mathrm{Lg}_\bundK(p_\bundK)\bigr).
\]
A predicate $A(\bundK,B,f,T)$ is called an \emph{at-property}.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}\label{pf:bundles:rem:closure}
The quantifiers $\mathrm{Ev},\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g},\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p},\sfname{EvLgG}$ are monotone and closed under
finite conjunctions, and $\mathrm{Ev}(p)$ implies $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g}(p)$ and $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}(p)$: for a
conjunction one takes the minimum of the two bounds $\eps_0$, the supremum of the two transported thresholds and the maxima
of the thresholds $c_1,c_2,x_0$, and for $\mathrm{Lg}$ the unions of the exceptional sets. These routine verifications are
used below without comment.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Column witnesses, stages, cores and slots.}
A \emph{column witness} $w$ for $(B,f,T)$ is a record of column input data whose frame is $f$ and whose characters range
over $\Xi(T,f)$, together with pins, column laws, a field datum for which $K_s$ is exact, and valuation conditions. It has a
type of \emph{levels} $Z$; each level determines a \emph{stage tuple} $X_w(Z)=(B,C,W,V,S,N,G)$ (columns, row rest, division
stage, $S$-stage, norm stage and volume--jet stage over $B$) and hence a global datum $D_w(Z)$ of
\cref{sec:families:theta}. A \emph{core} $\gamma$ over a stage tuple $X$ consists of the statement that the pre-base of $B$ lies
under $\bundK$, characters, a set $W_t$ of places, the forcing condition, vanishing conditions off $W_t$, an object
$\gamma.O$ over the datum of $X$ (with its norm choice $\gamma.O.\mathrm{nch}$, theta data $\gamma.O.\mathrm{xd}$ and column, row,
lattice and gauge layers) and compatibility conditions. A \emph{slot} is a predicate $Q(\bundK,X,f,T,\gamma)$ on cores; for
slots $Q,R$ we write $Q\wedge R$ for their conjunction and $Q^\exists(\bundK,B,f,T)$ for ``there are stages over $B$ and a
core $\gamma$ over them with $Q$''. A \emph{slot family} $\bundS$ consists of four families of types of requirements (on row,
norm-choice, spectral and object data).

\begin{definition}[Built and good cores]\label{pf:bundles:def:built}
Let $\bundS$ be a slot family, $w$ a column witness, $Z$ a level and $\gamma$ a core over $X_w(Z)$. A \emph{building} of
$\gamma$ for $\bundS$ consists of a column pin, coherence, column data at split primes, row data, elements of the four
requirement types of $\bundS$, kernel families, division, theta and object data, all compatible with $\gamma$.
$\mathrm{Built}_\bundS(w,Z,\gamma)$ means that there is a building whose kernel entries satisfy the \emph{entry condition} of
the development, and $\mathrm{Good}_\bundS(w,Z,\gamma)$ means $\mathrm{Built}_\bundS(w,Z,\gamma)$ together with the
\emph{goodness condition} on $(\gamma.O.\mathrm{nch},\gamma.O.\mathrm{xd})$ (existence of a good twist input and pairwise
disjointness of the twisting sets). For a predicate $P$ on $(\bundK,B,f,T,w,Z,\gamma)$ the slot $\mathrm{pos}(P)$ holds at
$(\bundK,X,f,T,\gamma)$ if there are a column witness $w$ for $(B,f,T)$, a level $Z$ and a core $\gamma'$ over $X_w(Z)$ with
$P(\bundK,B,f,T,w,Z,\gamma')$ such that $(X,\gamma)$ and $(X_w(Z),\gamma')$ coincide. Following \cref{def:families:rows},
\[
\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS)=\mathrm{pos}(\mathrm{Built}_\bundS),\qquad \sfname{IsGoodE}(\bundS)=\mathrm{pos}(\mathrm{Good}_\bundS).
\]
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Steps, passes and member existence.}
A \emph{base predicate} is a predicate $\kappa(B,f)$. Let $\kappa_0(B,f)$ be: there is a field datum for which $K_s$ is exact
with respect to $f$, and the pre-base of $B$ is pinned. The \emph{export condition} of a core $\gamma$ over $X$ consists of the
good-twist and Bloch--Kato conditions at the twisted places for the assembled datum of $X$ and
$(\gamma.O.\mathrm{nch},\gamma.O.\mathrm{xd})$.

\begin{definition}[Member existence]\label{pf:bundles:def:memberE}
Let $\kappa$ be a base predicate, $p,q,r$ at-properties and $Q,Q'$ slots.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p,q)=\sfname{EvLgG}\bigl(\kappa(B,f)\wedge T\text{ forced}\wedge p(\bundK,B,f,T)\Rightarrow
q(\bundK,B,f,T)\bigr)$.
\item The \emph{base condition} $\mathrm{BC}(\kappa)$: $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm g}$ of the statement that there are a pre-base $B_0$
under $\bundK$ and a frame $f_0$ such that for all finite sets $S_1,S_2$ of algebraic numbers (depending on the relevant
subfields) there is a base $B$ over $B_0$ with $\kappa(B,f_0)$, $S_1\subseteq\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}$ and $S_2\subseteq\mathit{LL}_\C$;
and moreover $\kappa(B,f)$ implies $\kappa(B',f)$ whenever $B\preceq B'$.
\item $\mathrm{Pass}(\kappa,Q,r)$: $\sfname{EvLgG}$ of the statement that if $\kappa(B,f)$ and $T=T^\circ$, then for every core
$\gamma$ over $B$ in $Q$ satisfying the export condition, every function $\mathcal A$ and every function $S_2$ assigning finite
sets of algebraic numbers to pre-bases, frames and sets of characters, there are a base $B'\succeq B$, the same frame and a
twisting datum $T'$ over $B'$ with $T'.\mathrm{pl}=T.\mathrm{pl}$, avoiding $\mathcal A$, such that
$S_2(B',f,\Xi(T',f))\subseteq\mathit{LL}'_\C$ and $r(\bundK,B',f,T')$.
\item For a slot family $\bundS$, $\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS)$ is the conjunction of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*)]
\item $\mathrm{BC}(\kappa_0)$;
\item $\mathrm{Step}\bigl(\kappa_0,\ T=T^\circ,\ \sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS)^\exists\bigr)$ (first step);
\item $\sfname{EvLgG}$ of: if $\kappa_0(B,f)$ and $T=T^\circ$, every core over $B$ in $\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS)$ satisfies the
export condition (export);
\item $\mathrm{Pass}\bigl(\kappa_0,\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS),\ (\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS)\wedge\mathrm{tw}\wedge
\sfname{IsGoodE}(\bundS))^\exists\bigr)$, where $\mathrm{tw}$ says that the norm choice of the core is twisted at all places
(second pass).
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

For predicates $p,q$ on $(\bundK,B,f,T,w)$ with $w$ a column witness let $[p\Rightarrow q]$ be the at-property ``$p\Rightarrow q$
holds at every column witness $w$ for $(B,f,T)$'', and put $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,q)=\mathrm{Step}(\kappa_0,\top,[p\Rightarrow q])$. A
witness is \emph{pinned} if it admits a column pin (a record of
choices of places over the twisting primes, jets and roots of unity in $\mathit{LL}$), and \emph{starting} if it is pinned,
$T$ is untwisted or the datum of $w$ is good at some level, and $(B,T)$ is coherent (places with the same valuations on
$\mathit{LL}_0$ carry the same twisting data, and $\mathit{LL}$ contains primitive $\ell_w^{k_w}$-th roots of unity).

\begin{lemma}[Composition of witness steps]\label{pf:bundles:lem:stepw}
Let $p,q,r$ be predicates on $(\bundK,B,f,T,w)$. If $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,q)$ and $\mathrm{Step}_W(q,r)$, then $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,r)$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.StepW.trans}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For a base predicate $\kappa$ and at-properties $p',q',r'$ the following rules hold: (i) $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',q')$ and
$\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',r')$ imply $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',q'\wedge r')$; (ii) $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',q')$ and
$\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,q',r')$ imply $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',r')$; (iii) if $p'\Rightarrow q'$ holds at every $(\bundK,B,f,T)$ such that the
pre-base of $B$ lies under $\bundK$, $\kappa(B,f)$ holds and $T$ is forced, then $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa,p',q')$. In (i) and (ii) the two
implications of the hypotheses hold simultaneously eventually and for large twisting data (\cref{pf:bundles:rem:closure}), and wherever
both hold, the implication of the conclusion holds; in (iii) the implication holds at every context, with empty exceptional sets. Now (i)
gives $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa_0,\top,[p\Rightarrow q]\wedge[q\Rightarrow r])$; the at-property $[p\Rightarrow q]\wedge[q\Rightarrow r]$
implies $[p\Rightarrow r]$ everywhere, so (iii) and (ii) give $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa_0,\top,[p\Rightarrow r])=\mathrm{Step}_W(p,r)$.
\end{proof}

In the same way, $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,q)$ and $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,r)$ imply $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,q\wedge r)$, and $\mathrm{Step}_W(p,q)$ holds
if $p\Rightarrow q$ at all witnesses. These rules are used below without comment.

\paragraph{Tuple ties.}
The columns of a stage tuple $X$ over $B$ carry a finite set of \emph{tuples}, among them $\mu_0,\mu^*,\mu^{*\prime}$ and a tuple
$\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w$ for every finite place $w$ of $\mathit{LL}$; the row rest of $X$ assigns to every place $w$ a tuple $\mu_w$, and
$\mu_{\bar w}=\mu_w$. For a frame $f$ and a twisting datum $T$ the \emph{twisted character} at $w$ is $\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w=\delta_{\ell_w}\psi_w$,
where $\delta_\ell=\delta'$ if $\ell$ is the auxiliary prime $p_{\mathrm{de}}$ of the host and $\delta_\ell=\delta$ otherwise; thus
$\Xi(T,f)=\{1,\delta,\delta'\}\cup\{\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w\}$. A \emph{norm choice} $n$ over the datum of $X$ assigns to every place $w$ a
finite set $Q^n_w$ of primes of $\OO_E$. A \emph{tuple tie} for $(X,f,n)$ is a pair $(\xi,\psi)$ of an injective map $\xi$ from the
tuples to Hecke characters of $E$ which have finite order, are tame at every place and have local components trivial on $F^\times$, and
of characters $\psi_w$ of $E$, such that $\xi(\mu_0)=1$, $\xi(\mu^*)=\delta$, $\xi(\mu^{*\prime})=\delta'$ and
$\xi(\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w)=\delta_{\ell_w}\psi_w$ for all $w$, $\psi_w$ is related to $(\ell_w,Q^n_w)$ by the condition of twisting data
whenever $\mu_w\neq\mu_0$, and $\psi_w=1$ whenever $\mu_w=\mu_0$.

\begin{lemma}[Twisted places of a tuple assignment]\label{pf:bundles:lem:tuptie}
Let $X$ be a stage tuple over $B$, $f$ a frame, $T$ a twisting datum, $W_t$ a set of places of $\mathit{LL}$ and $\xi$ a map from the
tuples of $X$ to Hecke characters of $E$. Assume that $\xi$ is injective with image $\Xi(T,f)$; that $\xi(\mu_0)=1$, $\xi(\mu^*)=\delta$,
$\xi(\mu^{*\prime})=\delta'$ and $\xi(\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w)=\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w$ for all $w$; that $\mu_w=\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w$ for $w\in W_t$
and $\mu_w=\mu_0$ for $w\notin W_t$; that $\ell_w\in T.\mathrm{pl}$ for $w\in W_t$; and that the characters $\psi_w$ of $T$ satisfy $\psi_w=1$
for $w\notin W_t$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\mu_w\neq\mu_0$ if and only if $w\in W_t$;
\item $W_t$ is finite and stable under complex conjugation;
\item for every norm choice $n$ with $Q^n_w=Q_w$ for all $w\in W_t$, the pair $(\xi,(\psi_w)_w)$, with the characters $\psi_w$ of $T$, is
a tuple tie for $(X,f,n)$.
\end{enumerate}
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.TupTieW.tupTied_of_muIsW}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The twisted characters $\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w$ are non-trivial, and only finitely many places of $\mathit{LL}$ have residue characteristic in
$T.\mathrm{pl}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.TwFin.tw_fin}, which proves these finiteness and non-triviality properties of twisting data).

(a) If $w\notin W_t$, then $\mu_w=\mu_0$ by hypothesis. If $w\in W_t$ and $\mu_w=\mu_0$, then $\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w=\mu_0$, so
$\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w=\xi(\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w)=\xi(\mu_0)=1$, which is impossible.

(b) $W_t$ is contained in the finite set of places with residue characteristic in $T.\mathrm{pl}$. If $w\in W_t$, then
$\mu_{\bar w}=\mu_w\neq\mu_0$ by (a), so $\bar w\in W_t$ by (a).

(c) The characters $1$, $\delta$, $\delta'$ and $\psi_w$ have finite order, are tame and have local components trivial on $F^\times$ (for
$\delta,\delta'$ by the axioms of a frame, for $\psi_w$ because it is a twist character), and these properties are stable under
products; hence every element of $\Xi(T,f)$, the image of $\xi$, has them. Injectivity and the values at $\mu_0,\mu^*,\mu^{*\prime}$ and
$\mu^{\mathrm{tw}}_w$ hold by hypothesis, as $\xi^{\mathrm{tw}}_w=\delta_{\ell_w}\psi_w$. If $\mu_w\neq\mu_0$, then $w\in W_t$ by (a), so
$Q^n_w=Q_w$, and $\psi_w$ is related to $(\ell_w,Q_w)$ because $T$ is a twisting datum. If $\mu_w=\mu_0$, then $w\notin W_t$ by (a), and
$\psi_w=1$ by hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Parameters and norm-choice extensions.}
A \emph{norm-choice extension} $N$ assigns to every configuration (a context, base, frame, twisting datum, column witness $w$,
level $Z$, set $W_t$ of places, base-level row side, rows, norm choice with its data and theta data) and every norm-choice
core over it a type of extra data. A \emph{parameter} consists of a row position, a norm-choice extension $N$, a spectral
extension and a geometric predicate $\Gamma$; its slot family has the row requirement of the row position, the norm-choice
requirement ``an element of $N$ exists'', the spectral requirement of the spectral extension and the object requirement of
$\Gamma$. We write $\bundp(N,\Gamma)$ for the parameter with the fixed row position of the development, no spectral extension,
the extension $N$ and the geometric predicate $\Gamma$, and $\bundS(N,\Gamma)$ for its slot family. The extensions used are:
\begin{itemize}
\item $N_{\mathrm{law}}$: the default extension together with the \emph{law} of the datum $D_w(Z)$ (if the values of the
rational maps of the datum are rational, then so are the values of its Bessel functionals $B_u$);
\item $N_{\mathrm{list}}(c)$, for $c\in\R$: $N_{\mathrm{law}}$ together with a list pin at $(c,b)$, where $b$ is the scale
quantity of $\bundK$;
\item the origin extension $N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)$, built from $h_L\in\sfname{RecTExists}$ (\cref{def:rectexists}):
$N_{\mathrm{list}}(c)$ together with \emph{origin data}: a pre-datum $\mathcal P$ over $w$, a state $x$ of $\mathcal P$ which is
listed for $c$, with $\mathcal P$ saturated, a strict-henselian acyclic level of $x$ whose character choice keeps the given norm
choice, and $W_t=\mathcal P.W_t$ (pre-data and states are recalled in \cref{pf:bundles:saturated});
\item $N\times A$, for an at-property $A$: $N$ together with a proof of $A(\bundK,B,f,T)$.
\end{itemize}
Let $\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}}$ be the closure of the trivial geometric predicate. The parameter of \cref{thm:member-bundles} is
$\bundp_A=\bundp(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)\times A,\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$. We say that $N$ \emph{yields} $N'$ if every element of $N$ at
a configuration yields an element of $N'$ at the same configuration; $N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)$ and $N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)\times A$
yield $N_{\mathrm{law}}$.

\paragraph{Weighted points, bundles and linkage.}
For a slot family $\bundS$ and a context $\bundK$, a \emph{witness} at $(w,Z,O)$, $O$ an object over $D_w(Z)$, records: the
pre-base lies under $\bundK$, the forcing condition, a set $W_t$, a column pin, coherence, a volume tie, column data at split

real ties and the goodness condition. A \emph{weighted point} $q$ is a tuple $(B,f,T,w,Z,O)$ with such a witness; we write

layer whose object is the object of the core and numerical conditions (size data at the scale of $\bundK$, weights, validity,
goodness of $\bundK$, reciprocity and ledger conditions, use of all tuples, twisting at all places); it determines a datum
$D_\beta$ and an object $O_\beta$, and $\beta$ \emph{lies in} a slot $Q$ if $Q$ holds at its core.

\begin{definition}[Bundles and bundle existence]\label{pf:bundles:def:exbl}
For a datum $D$, an object $O$ over $D$ and $a\in\R$, a \emph{bundle} $b\in\mathrm{Bd}(D,O,a)$ consists of a reciprocity record

the compatibility of the record with $q$, the identification of the spectral ties with the saturation of $q$, and further ties
between the realisation of the bundle and the data of $q$. For slots $Q,Q',R$, a base predicate $\kappa$ and a linkage
$\mathrm{Lk}$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item the \emph{large-base condition} $\mathrm{LB}(\kappa,Q,R)$ is $\mathrm{Ev}\,\mathrm{Lg}$ of: if $\kappa(B,f)$, every core over
$B$ in $Q$ lies in $R$; its variant $\mathrm{LB}^{\mathrm g}(\kappa,Q,R)$ uses $\sfname{EvLgG}$ instead;
\item \emph{bundle existence} $\mathrm{BE}(Q,\mathrm{Lk})$ is $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}$ of: for every bundle point $\beta$ in $Q$ and every
$a\in\R$ there is $b\in\mathrm{Bd}(D_\beta,O_\beta,a)$ with $\mathrm{Lk}(\bundK,\beta,a,b)$;
\item $\sfname{ExBL}(\kappa,Q,Q',\mathrm{Lk})$: there is a slot $R$ with $\mathrm{LB}(\kappa,Q,R)$ and $\mathrm{BE}(Q'\wedge R,\mathrm{Lk})$.
\end{enumerate}
With $\bundS_A=\bundS(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)\times A,\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$ the slot family of $\bundp_A$, the bundle statement of

\end{definition}

\subsection{The Siegel mass ratio}\label{pf:bundles:mass}

Let $K$ be a number field with an involution $a\mapsto\bar a$ and $V$ a $K$-vector space with a sesquilinear form $h$. The
isometry group $\Uni(h)$ consists of the $K$-linear automorphisms preserving $h$. Two $\OO_K$-lattices $L,M\subset V$ are in the
same \emph{genus} if for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak p$ of $\OO_K$ there is $g\in\Uni(h)$ with $g(L_{(\mathfrak p)})=M_{(\mathfrak
p)}$, where $L_{(\mathfrak p)}$ denotes the localisation. A finite set $s$ of lattices is a set of \emph{class representatives} of
the genus of $L$ if every element of $s$ lies in the genus and every lattice of the genus is the image under $\Uni(h)$ of exactly one
element of $s$. The \emph{mass} is
\[
\mathrm{mass}(h,L)=\sum_{M\in s}\frac{1}{|\{g\in\Uni(h):g(M)=M\}|}
\]
for such a set $s$ (chosen once and for all), and $\mathrm{mass}(h,L)=0$ if no finite set of class representatives exists. Write
$\mathrm{mass}_M$ for the mass of the standard lattice $\OO_K^M$ with the form $h_M(x,y)=\sum_i\bar x_iy_i$.

\begin{proposition}[\cref{prop:families:mass}]\label{pf:bundles:prop:mass}
Let $K$ be a totally complex number field with an involution such that $\varphi(\bar a)=\overline{\varphi(a)}$ for every embedding
$\varphi\colon K\to\C$ and all $a\in K$, and such that for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak P$ of $\OO_K$ there is $\theta\in\OO_K$
with $\theta-\bar\theta\notin\mathfrak P$. Let $d$ be the number of infinite places of $K$, $v=|\mathrm{disc}\,K|^{1/(2d)}/2$ and
$M\ge3$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:mass}
\frac{\mathrm{mass}_M}{\mathrm{mass}_{M-1}}=\frac{\bigl((M-1)!\,(v/\pi)^M\bigr)^{d}}{\sqrt{(2v)^{d}}}
\prod_{\mathfrak P}e_M(\mathfrak P),\quad
e_M(\mathfrak P)=\begin{cases}\bigl(1-(-1)^M\Nm\mathfrak P^{-M/2}\bigr)^{-1},&\bar{\mathfrak P}=\mathfrak P,\\
\bigl(1-\Nm\mathfrak P^{-M}\bigr)^{-1/2},&\bar{\mathfrak P}\ne\mathfrak P,\end{cases}
\end{equation}
the product (an unconditional infinite product) running over the maximal ideals of $\OO_K$, and $\bar{\mathfrak P}$ denoting the
ideal generated by the conjugates of the elements of $\mathfrak P$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mass.massRatio_all_unramified_cmFields}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By \lref{GlobalTheory.Mass.siegelMassRatio_holds}, proved elsewhere in this part, the following holds for every field $K$ as in
the statement and every $M\ge3$: if $h,h'$ are hermitian forms on $K$-vector spaces $V,V'$, and $L\subset V$, $L'\subset V'$ are the
$\OO_K$-spans of $K$-bases $(e_i)_{i<M}$ of $V$, resp.\ $(e'_i)_{i<M-1}$ of $V'$, with $h(e_i,e_j)=\delta_{ij}$ and
$h'(e'_i,e'_j)=\delta_{ij}$, then $\mathrm{mass}(h,L)/\mathrm{mass}(h',L')$ equals the right-hand side of \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:mass}.
The standard form $h_M$ is hermitian, and the standard basis of $K^M$ is orthonormal for $h_M$ and spans $\OO_K^M$; the same holds for
$M-1$. Applying the quoted statement to $(h_M,\OO_K^M)$ and $(h_{M-1},\OO_K^{M-1})$ gives the proposition (this specialisation is
\lref{Formalism.MainNotions.StandardLattice.standardMassRatio_of_modelMassRatio}, which states the case of standard lattices of
the general ratio).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}\label{pf:bundles:rem:mass}
(a) The hypotheses say that $K$ is a CM field whose involution is complex conjugation and that $K$ is unramified over its maximal
totally real subfield $K^+$ at all finite primes; $d=[K^+:\Q]$. (b) In the formal statement the infinite product is the unconditional
product of the library of the development; for $M\ge3$ the product $\prod_{\mathfrak P}e_M(\mathfrak P)$ converges absolutely. It is
the value $L(M,\eta^M)$ of the Hecke $L$-function of the $M$-th power of the quadratic character $\eta$ of $K/K^+$, written as a
product over the primes of $K$. (c) This is a ratio form of Siegel's mass formula for unimodular hermitian lattices
\cite{Siegel1935,Prasad1989,GanHankeYu2001}; we have not compared the normalisations with the published ones. (d) In the proof of
\cref{thm:member-bundles} the proposition is one of the components of the package $\mathcal H_A$ (\cref{pf:bundles:def:packages}); it is a
theorem and does not depend on the hypotheses of that theorem. (e) In the definition of the mass, the cardinality of an infinite set is $0$ in
the library of the development and $1/0$ is read as $0$; for the totally positive forms $h_M$ the stabilisers of lattices are finite, so this
convention plays no role here.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Meromorphy of doubling zeta integrals]\label{pf:bundles:lem:zetacont}
Let $V$ be a hermitian space over $E$, $\iota\colon\Uni(V)\times\Uni(V)\to\Uni(V\oplus(-V))$ the doubling embedding, $\mu$ a unitary
Hecke character, $m$ a Haar measure on $\Uni(V)(\A_F)$, and $f_1,f_2$ automorphic functions on $\Uni(V)(\A_F)$ of rapid decay. For a
function $\mathcal E$ on $\Uni(V\oplus(-V))(\A_F)$ put
\[
\mathcal Z(\mathcal E)=\int\!\!\int_{(\Uni(V)(F)\backslash\Uni(V)(\A_F))^2}\mathcal E(\iota(g_1,g_2))\,\overline{f_1(g_1)}\,f_2(g_2)\,
\mu(\det g_2)^{-1}\,dm(g_1)\,dm(g_2).
\]
Let $\Phi(s)$ be a family of sections whose Eisenstein series $\mathcal E(s)$ has a continuation $\mathcal E^*(s)$, left invariant
under the rational points, such that $s\mapsto\mathcal E(s)(g)$ is analytic on $\Re s>\rank V/2$ for every $g$, and such that
$\mathcal E^*$ is regular of finite order everywhere: for every $s_0\in\C$ there are $k\in\N$, $r>0$ and a function $H(s,g)$ on
$B(s_0,r)\times\Uni(V\oplus(-V))(\A_F)$, holomorphic in $s$, jointly continuous, with $|H(s,g)|\le C\,\mathrm{ht}(g)^N$ for some $C,N$,
and $H(s,\cdot)=(s-s_0)^k\mathcal E^*(s)$ for $s\neq s_0$. If the integrals $\mathcal Z(\mathcal E(s))$ converge for $\Re s>\rank V/2$,
then $s\mapsto\mathcal Z(\mathcal E(s))$ extends to a meromorphic function on $\C$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Eis.zetaContinues_of_eisRegularAt}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
There are $C',c'$ with $\mathrm{ht}(\iota(g_1,g_2))\le C'(\mathrm{ht}(g_1)\mathrm{ht}(g_2))^{c'}$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_height_doublingEmbedding_le_pow_mul_heights}, a polynomial bound for the height of the doubling
embedding). Consequently, for all $C,N$ there is a function $\beta$, integrable on the square of the automorphic quotient, which
bounds the integrand of $\mathcal Z(\mathcal E)$ for every left-invariant $\mathcal E$ with $|\mathcal E(g)|\le C\,\mathrm{ht}(g)^N$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_integrable_bound_doubling_integrand_quotient}, which states this domination for functions of
rapid decay $f_1,f_2$).

\emph{Claim.} If $H(s,\cdot)$, $s\in B(z,r)$, is a family of left-invariant functions, holomorphic in $s$, jointly continuous and
bounded by $C\,\mathrm{ht}^N$ uniformly in $s$, then $\mathcal Z(H(s,\cdot))$ converges for $s\in B(z,r)$ and is holomorphic there.
Indeed, the integrands are measurable (they are continuous, and the integral over the quotient is computed through a measurable
section), holomorphic in $s$, and dominated by the single integrable function $\beta$; the theorem on holomorphy of parameter
integrals with locally dominated holomorphic integrands
(\lref{GlobalTheory.differentiableOn_integral_of_holomorphic_of_locally_dominated}) gives the claim.

Put $Z(s)=\mathcal Z(\mathcal E^*(s))$. Fix $s_1$ and let $k,r,H$ be as in the hypothesis. The functions $H(s,\cdot)$ are left
invariant: for $s\ne s_1$ because $\mathcal E^*(s)$ is, and for $s=s_1$ by continuity in $s$. By the claim, $s\mapsto\mathcal
Z(H(s,\cdot))$ is holomorphic near $s_1$, and for $s\neq s_1$ near $s_1$ we have $\mathcal E^*(s)=(s-s_1)^{-k}H(s,\cdot)$, hence
$Z(s)=(s-s_1)^{-k}\mathcal Z(H(s,\cdot))$ by linearity of $\mathcal Z$. Thus $Z$ is meromorphic at every point. For $\Re s>\rank V/2$
the continuation agrees with the Eisenstein series (both are analytic there and agree on a non-empty open set), so
$Z(s)=\mathcal Z(\mathcal E(s))$ there, and $Z$ is the required meromorphic extension.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Doubling zeta integrals]\label{pf:bundles:lem:dm}
Rapid decay of cusp forms and the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series imply the \emph{doubling package}: for every global
datum, hermitian space $V$ of positive rank, majorant, Hecke character $\mu$ satisfying the residue condition of the hypothesis and
section $\varphi$ of the degenerate principal series, (i) the Eisenstein series of the flat section of $\varphi$ continues and is
regular of finite order at every point; (ii) the true proposition; and (iii) for every pair $f_1,f_2$ of cusp forms (with respect to a
Cartan frame), the basic identity of the doubling method holds for $\Re s>\rank V/2$ and the doubling zeta integral of $(f_1,f_2)$
continues meromorphically to $\C$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.doubling_zeta_of_eisenstein_facts}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Statement (i) is the hypothesis on Siegel Eisenstein series. Cusp forms are of rapid decay by the first hypothesis
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Grow.rapidDec_of_cuspForm}, which deduces rapid decay of cusp forms from it). For $\Re s>\rank V/2$ the
Eisenstein series of the flat section has moderate growth
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.siegelEisenstein_spherical_moderateGrowth_real_point}); hence the doubling integral of two rapidly
decreasing cusp forms converges absolutely and satisfies the basic identity of the doubling method of Piatetski-Shapiro and Rallis
\cite{PiatetskiShapiroRallis1987} (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.doubling_basicIdentity_unfolding}, which proves the unfolding
identity in this form). The continuation of the zeta integral is \cref{pf:bundles:lem:zetacont}, whose hypotheses are (i), the
analyticity of the Eisenstein series in its region of convergence and the convergence just proved. The formal statement has a third
hypothesis, the component of $P$ named after the simple pole of Siegel Eisenstein series; its type is the true proposition and it is
not used.
\end{proof}

A(\bundK,B,f,T),

and the same holds with $\sfname{IsGoodE}$ in place of $\sfname{IsBuiltE}$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mem.LiteralAutomorphy.isBuiltE_extensionWithAtProperty_iff}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A building of $\gamma$ for $\bundS(N\times A,\Gamma)$ differs from a building for $\bundS(N,\Gamma)$ only in its norm-choice requirement,
which for $N\times A$ is a pair consisting of an element of $N$ and a proof of $A(\bundK,B,f,T)$. Dropping the second component turns a
building for $N\times A$ into one for $N$ with the same kernel entries, and conversely a building for $N$ and a proof of $A$ give a
building for $N\times A$. The goodness condition does not involve the requirement. The equivalences follow by unfolding the definition of
$\mathrm{pos}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Adding an at-property]\label{pf:bundles:lem:atprop}
If $\sfname{EvLgG}(A)$ and $\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS(N,\Gamma))$, then $\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS(N\times A,\Gamma))$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.memberE_extensionWithAtProperty_of_evLgG}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $Q=\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS(N,\Gamma))$, $Q'=\sfname{IsGoodE}(\bundS(N,\Gamma))$, and $Q_A$, $Q'_A$ for the corresponding slots of
$N\times A$. We check (M1)--(M4) of \cref{pf:bundles:def:memberE}. The base condition (M1) does not involve the slot family.

(M2) $\sfname{EvLgG}(A)$ implies $\mathrm{Step}(\kappa_0,p,A)$ for every $p$. By (M2) for $N$ and closure under conjunction, eventually and
for large twisting data the following holds: if $\kappa_0(B,f)$ and $T$ is forced and untwisted, then there is a core $\gamma$ over $B$ in
$Q$, and $A(\bundK,B,f,T)$ holds. By \cref{pf:bundles:lem:builtiff}, $\gamma$ lies in $Q_A$.

(M3) A core in $Q_A$ lies in $Q$ by \cref{pf:bundles:lem:builtiff}; hence (M3) for $N$ implies (M3) for $N\times A$, with the same thresholds
and exceptional sets.

(M4) Let $R$ be the slot ``$A(\bundK,B,f,T)$ holds''. The statement $\sfname{EvLgG}(A)$ implies $\mathrm{LB}^{\mathrm g}(\kappa_0,Q,R)$.
By \lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.GroupRetwisting.SecondG.sAnd_of_largeBase}, which states that the base condition, a large-base
condition $\mathrm{LB}^{\mathrm g}(\kappa,Q,R)$ and the second pass $\mathrm{Pass}(\kappa,Q,(Q\wedge\mathrm{tw}\wedge Q')^\exists)$ imply
$\mathrm{Pass}(\kappa,Q,(Q\wedge\mathrm{tw}\wedge Q'\wedge R)^\exists)$, we obtain the latter for $\kappa=\kappa_0$. Now let $\gamma$ be a core in
$Q_A$ satisfying the export condition, and let $\mathcal A,S_2$ be given. Then $\gamma$ lies in $Q$, and the pass gives an enlarged base
$B'$, the same frame, a twisting datum $T'$ with the same prime set, avoiding $\mathcal A$ and realising $S_2$, and a core $\gamma'$ over
$B'$ which lies in $Q$, in $Q'$ and in $R$ and is twisted at all places. Since $A(\bundK,B',f,T')$ holds, \cref{pf:bundles:lem:builtiff}
shows that $\gamma'$ lies in $Q_A$ and in $Q'_A$. This is (M4) for $N\times A$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Existence of linked bundles}\label{pf:bundles:exbl}

In this subsection $h_L$ is fixed, $N$ is a norm-choice extension which yields $N_{\mathrm{law}}$, $\Gamma$ is a geometric predicate,
$\bundp=\bundp(N,\Gamma)$ and $\bundS=\bundS(N,\Gamma)$.

\paragraph{Data at weighted points.}
Let $q$ be a weighted point for a slot family. Its witness contains rows $t_q$, which carry a real datum $z_q$ (\cref{pf:bundles:saturated})
with face $F_q$, together with a column pin and the object $O_q$ with its extra data (theta data, kernel family, norm choice and twisting
inputs). A datum $D$ is \emph{level tied} if the level primes of its host are exactly $g_0,g_1,p_{\mathrm{de}},p'_{\mathrm{de}}$, the
primes $T_u$ attached to the words $u$, and $q_0$. We use the following records of the development.
\begin{itemize}
\item A \emph{face datum} $(D_c,\mathrm{Sp},Z_{\mathrm{sc}})$ consists of column definitions $D_c$ over the defining subspaces of the
ambient family of $t_q$, spectral periods $\mathrm{Sp}$ for them and a scale $Z_{\mathrm{sc}}$; \emph{re-facing} replaces the face of $z_q$
by it. A \emph{column reading} of a re-faced real datum consists of a word map $w_U$, a bijection and an eigen-normalisation $e_\nu$, read
off from the columns.
\item \emph{Twisted arguments} for $O_q$ and $t_q$ consist of a \emph{twisted input} $x$ (columns with a column reading, real data, a face
with column definitions, spectral periods and a scale, and an evaluation map) together with ties of $x$ to the rows $t_q$; the \emph{division
pair} of $x$ is the pair of adelic forms attached to the face columns of $x$.
\item \emph{Tied data} over $O_q$ are spectral data together with a kernel. For tied data and a division pair, \emph{square-integrable columns}
are columns with a face, spectral periods, weights and characters whose column functions are square-integrable; their column characters are
\emph{compatible} if they agree with the characters of the tied data.
\item \emph{Spectral data compatible with $q$} are tied spectral data whose host, columns, theta data, twisting inputs, normalised characters
with their tuple tie, Galois data, exponents, types, cover, geometry and twisting are those of $q$, and whose class field pieces are the images
of the ray class field and of the field $M_\Delta$ of the column witness. A reciprocity record $R$ over the extra data of $O_q$ is
\emph{compatible with $q$} if its spectral data are compatible with $q$, its kernel is pinned to that of $q$, its coordinates are $1$, the
kernel of $q$ satisfies the entry condition, and the kernel of $R$ is standard and read in the standard way.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lemma}[Square-integrable columns]\label{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols}
Let $q$ be a weighted point for a slot family and $\tau$ a tied kernel entry of $O_q$ which is pinned for $q$, and assume that the rows
$t_q$ admit a row extension. There are a face datum $(D_c,\mathrm{Sp}_0,Z_{\mathrm{sc},0})$ and a column reading $C_0$ of the re-faced real
datum of $q$ such that for all tied data $\mathcal T$ with kernel $\tau$ and all twisted arguments with input $x$ whose column definitions are
$D_c$ and whose column reading agrees with $C_0$ in $w_U$ and $e_\nu$, there are square-integrable columns for $\mathcal T$ and the division
pair of $x$ whose face and spectral periods are those of $x$, whose weights are those of the base and whose characters are those of $z_q$, with
compatible column characters.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelC.square_integrable_columns_of_witnesses}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
From a row extension, the column pin and $\tau$ one obtains the column record of the witness with kernel $\tau$; its face, with trivial
spectral periods and the scale of the face of $z_q$, is a face datum, and there is a column reading $C_0$ of the re-faced real datum whose
word map is the identity (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelC.exists_readC_wU_eq_id}, which constructs this reading from the column
record). Let
$\mathcal T$ and $x$ be as in the statement. The components of the twisted arguments agree with those of the column record of the witness at the
defining subspace of $z_q$, the column definitions of $x$ are $D_c$, and the word map of $x$ is the identity. Hence the tied column construction
applies (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelC.exists_rowsKC_colPins_alt}, which builds pinned columns for tied data with a standard kernel
from these agreements); it gives columns for $\mathcal T$ and the division pair of $x$ with the face and spectral periods of $x$, the weights of
the base, the characters of $z_q$ and compatible column characters. Both constructions rest on the column-factor hypotheses of twisted column
inputs (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Col2.TC.RcHyp.hyps_of_isTw}: the column factors and the evaluation datum at the base tuple are admissible,
the index matrices are in normal form, and the kernel equivalences hold). The columns so obtained, regarded as square-integrable columns, are
as required.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{pf:bundles:def:zeta}
A datum $D$ is \emph{zeta compatible} if for every word $u$, every tuple $\mu$ and every $z\in\C$ the following holds: if the period
columns of $D$ satisfy $\mathrm{per}(u,\mu,\varphi,F)=z\,B_u\bigl(\varphi,\iota_\mu(\mathrm{pr}_\mu F)\bigr)$ for all $\varphi$ and all
$F$ in the space $V_0(\mu)$, where $B_u$ is the Bessel functional of $u$, $\mathrm{pr}_\mu$ the orthogonal projection onto the
$\mu$-part and $\iota_\mu$ the corresponding inclusion, and some period column $\mathrm{per}(u,\mu,\varphi,F)$ with $F\in V_0(\mu)$ is
non-zero, then the recorded zeta constant $\zeta_D(u,\mu)$ equals $z$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Zeta constants at weighted points]\label{pf:bundles:lem:zeta}
Assume the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Harish-Chandra finiteness and rapid decay. Then for
every context and every weighted point $q$ for $\bundS$ (with any spectral extension), the datum $D_q$ is zeta compatible.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.zeta_uniform_of_extension'}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The kernel family of the witness of $q$ has a tied entry $\tau$ which is standard and pinned
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Col2.TC.kerEntTied_true_ent}, which produces such an entry from the kernel, division and object data of a
witness). The witness also contains a row extension, so \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols} gives a face datum $(D_c,\mathrm{Sp}_0,Z_{\mathrm{sc},0})$
and a column reading $C_0$ of the re-faced real datum. Re-facing the real data of the rows along $D_c$ and $\mathrm{Sp}_0$
gives scalars, errors and readings (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.reFace_scalars_errors_readings}) and an updated reading of the
columns (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.readC_reFace_updS}). Since $N$ yields $N_{\mathrm{law}}$, the norm-choice requirement of $q$
provides the law of $D_q$. The division pairing is rational on rational points
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.divisionPairing_rational_on_rationalPoints}), so by the law the Bessel values are rational, which is
the hypothesis of the reading lemma \lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.zeTied_of_read}; that lemma concludes that the recorded zeta constants
are the proportionality constants of \cref{pf:bundles:def:zeta}, i.e.\ that $D_q$ is zeta compatible.
\end{proof}

normalised tied data whose Galois data, exponents, characters, types, cover and places agree with the core data of $q$, with good twisting inputs and
with the character frame and tuple tie of $q$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.exists_core_parameters_agree}, which reads them off the core
of $q$). The twisting datum of $O_q$ is that of the column witness at the level of $q$, and it is standard and tied to the column data
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.WPt.colsTwTied}), and the $D_t$-components can be tied to the classes of the given elements
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Col2.TC.dtTied_true}). Global Artin reciprocity and Weber's ray class fields realise the class field pieces inside

\begin{lemma}[Selected realisations]\label{pf:bundles:lem:selreal}
Assume the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Weil representations of unitary pairs, existence of discrete
series, Harish-Chandra finiteness, rapid decay, global Artin reciprocity and ray class fields. Let $q$ be a weighted point for $\bundS$ over a

package and spectral rest of the saturation of $q$ (for the forms of $R$), and $M$ the real spectral matching of $R$ and $S$; assume that the
period vectors of $M$ lie in its Hilbert space. Then for all $\Delta,h_0\in\N$ and $c\in\R$ there are twisted arguments with input $x$ and
square-integrable columns for the tied data of $R$ and the division pair of $x$, with the properties stated in \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols},
such that moreover the scale of the face of $x$ is the scale of the division pair of $x$ determined by $(\Delta,h_0,c)$, the spectral periods of
$x$ at its scale point are those read off from $M$, and for some $\kappa>0$ the evaluation map of $x$ is $\kappa$ times the standard evaluation
on the vectors of the level part.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.exists_selected_realization_alt}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The parameter $\bundp$ has no spectral extension, so the witness of $q$ contains a row extension. The kernel of $R$ is pinned to that of $q$,
and \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols}, applied to this kernel, gives a face datum $(D_c,\mathrm{Sp}_0,Z_{\mathrm{sc},0})$ and a column reading $C_0$
with the property stated there. Further:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item by Weil representations, existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra finiteness, rapid decay and $\mathrm{EM}(R_q)$, at every place
where the third-level eigenspace of $z_q$ is non-zero all the row spaces of $z_q$ are non-zero and the row parts are present at every place
of the family other than its base place (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelectionPairing.wp3_ne_bot_and_piece_at_of_multEq}, a consequence of the
row-part theorem for real data with equal multiplicities and good generic characters);
\item the saturation of $q$ provides an evaluation tie between the base forms of $z_q$ and the Hida setting of $P$, with an element $g$ and a
constant $c_q>0$; since the forms of $R$ are the column forms of $q$, it is an evaluation tie for the package $P$ of the statement
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelectionPairing.ev_at_join_pinned});
\item the selection construction (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.SelectionPairing.selection_group_ambient_tie}, which uses the discrete series
cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Harish-Chandra finiteness and rapid decay, and builds twisted arguments from the ambient and
group ties of the witness, a face datum with a column reading, an evaluation tie and period weights) applied with (i), (ii), the face datum and
$C_0$ of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols}, the period weights of $M$ and the size data of $O_q$, gives twisted arguments with input $x$ whose column
definitions are $D_c$, whose column reading agrees with $C_0$ in $w_U$ and $e_\nu$, whose face scale is the scale determined by
$(\Delta,h_0,c)$, whose spectral periods at the scale point are read off from $M$, and whose evaluation map is $\kappa$ times the standard
evaluation on the level part for some $\kappa>0$.
\end{enumerate}
The conclusion of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sqcols}, applied to the tied data of $R$ (whose kernel is the given one) and $x$, gives the
square-integrable columns.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The realisation side]\label{pf:bundles:lem:selside}
Assume Green's cuspidal characters and the hypotheses of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:selreal}, and let $q,R,P,S,M$ be as there. For all twisted
arguments with input $x$ and every real input $y$ of $x$ for $R$ and $M$ there is a recursive input over $y$ whose realisation recursion has
its rows partitioned into parts.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.selected_realization_side_alt}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The twisted arguments carry the level data of $q$: the character frame of the real data of $x$ is the column frame of the witness, and the
tuple characters of $x$ at the used tuples are those of the witness (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.twArgs_lev}); and the two readings required
of a recursive input (of the twisted pairing of the division pair of $x$, and of the realisation of $y$) hold by the primitive row conditions of
$x$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.Reads.mt_mj_read_alt}). Since $R$ is compatible with $q$, its character frame and tuple tie
are those of $q$, whose characters are those of the witness. Hence the frame of the real data of $x$ is that of $R$, and the tuple tie of $R$,
transported to this frame, agrees with the tuple characters of $x$ at the used tuples.

The parts theorem (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.partsRow_of_eigensystemIndex_goodCharacters}: under Weil representations, existence of discrete
series, Harish-Chandra finiteness and rapid decay, real data with pins, primitive rows and the host hypotheses admit an eigensystem index, labels
and local parameters for which the rows are partitioned into parts, provided every character of the family has non-zero product of its
idempotent with the level idempotent, and the logarithmic eigenvalues of the division datum on the isotypic parts are the logarithms of the
third-level eigenvalues of the real data at $\mu_0$) applies to the real data of $x$. Indeed, the primitive row conditions are part of the
twisted arguments; the host hypotheses hold at the context; the idempotent condition holds by reciprocity
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.geometric_parameter_of_reciprocity_alt}, which deduces it from the reciprocity data of the weighted point); and the
eigenvalue condition holds by discrete-series reciprocity (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.parameter_of_discrete_series_reciprocity_alt}, which
identifies the eigenvalues of the division datum with those of the real data, using Green's cuspidal characters). The eigensystem index,
labels and parameters so obtained, with the transported tuple tie and the readings of $y$, form a recursive input over $y$ whose realisation
recursion is the parts recursion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Bundle witnesses]\label{pf:bundles:lem:bwit}
Assume Green's cuspidal characters, the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Weil representations of
unitary pairs, existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra finiteness, rapid decay, global Artin reciprocity and ray class fields. If
$\mathrm{Mult}(\bundp)$ holds, then the following holds $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}$: for every weighted point $q$ for $\bundS$, every
reciprocity record $R$ compatible with $q$, every $a\in\R$ and every $(\Delta,h)\in\N^2$ with $D_q.s.\Delta=\Delta$ and $\eta$-size of
$O_q$ equal to $mh$, if the size data of $O_q$ have the scale of $\bundK$ and are valid for its constants, there is a bundle
$b\in\mathrm{Bd}(D_q,O_q,a)$ with $b.R=R$ and a bundle witness for $(q,b)$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.local_langlands_prm_reduction_alt}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We work at a context at which the host hypotheses hold (an eventual statement,
\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.HostFrames.hostHyp_frames_ev}) and $\mathrm{EM}$ holds at all weighted points. The columns of $R$ are those
of the witness of $q$, so the saturation at the weighted point (part of the data of $q$) provides a spectral package and a spectral rest
for the forms of $R$; the period vectors of the real spectral matching of $R$ and the spectral rest lie in its Hilbert space (a
finiteness property of the spectral package). From these data a selected realisation is constructed in five steps:
twisted inputs with their arguments, square-integrable columns at the face of the inputs, and spectral, zeta and Bessel data matching
the constraints of a bundle (\cref{pf:bundles:lem:selreal}); its geometric part, a realisation of the input
with the ties to the point required in a bundle witness (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.selected_realization_geometric_alt}); its realisation
side, a recursion whose rows are partitioned into parts (\cref{pf:bundles:lem:selside}); the lower map pin
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.selected_lower_map_holds_alt}); and the selected side of the realisation
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.selected_side_realization_holds_alt}), with a host datum from
\lref{GlobalTheory.HostDatumExists.exists_hostDatum_alt}. The bundle $b$ consists of $R$, the real spectral matching obtained from
the spectral rest, the division pair of the adelic forms of the inputs, the pin, $(\Delta,h)$, the columns and the bounded realisation;
the ties between the selected data and $b$ are identities between the constructions. These data form a bundle witness for $(q,b)$.
\end{proof}

\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.eta_size_recursion_alt}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Take for $R$ the slot ``true''; the large-base condition $\mathrm{LB}(\kappa_0,\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS'),R)$ holds trivially. It remains
to prove bundle existence. By closure of $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}$ under conjunction we may fix $\kappa$ and a context $\bundK$ of $\kappa$
at which both $\mathrm{Ev}^{\mathrm p}$ hypotheses hold. Let $\beta$ be a bundle point in $\sfname{IsGoodE}(\bundS')$ and $a\in\R$.

By $(\mathrm{WE})$ there is a weighted point $q$ with $(D_q,O_q)=(D_\beta,O_\beta)$ whose kernel satisfies the entry condition. Since
$\beta$ is a bundle point, the context is $(C_5,c_6,C_b)$-good for some constants; in particular the level primes of the host of the context are
$\{g_0,g_1,p_{\mathrm{de}},p'_{\mathrm{de}},q_0\}$. The core of $\beta$ lies over the pre-base of the context, so the level primes of the host of
$D_\beta$ are those of the context together with the primes $T_u$ of the words; thus $D_\beta$ is level tied
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.HD.levEx_of_over}, which proves this description of the level primes of a datum over a context), hence so is
$D_q$. \Cref{pf:bundles:lem:rec} gives a
reciprocity record $R'$ compatible with $q$. The size data of the layer of $\beta$ are valid and have the scale of $\bundK$, and validity
gives $(\Delta,h)\in\N^2$ with $D_\beta.s.\Delta=\Delta$ and $\eta$-size $mh$. The conclusion of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:bwit} gives a
bundle $b'$ at $q$ with $b'.R=R'$ and a bundle witness. Transporting $R'$ and $b'$ along the identification of $(D_q,O_q)$ with
$(D_\beta,O_\beta)$ gives $b\in\mathrm{Bd}(D_\beta,O_\beta,a)$ with $\sfname{BundleLinkage}(\bundp')(\bundK,\beta,a,b)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Linked bundles exist]\label{pf:bundles:prop:exbl}
Assume Green's cuspidal characters, the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Weil representations of

\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.geometric_parameter_uniform_alt}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
We verify the hypotheses of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:exblred}. Movement of positions implies movement of witnesses
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.witnesses_pass_along_set_division}, which states $(\mathrm{Mv})\Rightarrow(\mathrm{WM})$ for every slot family).

the entry condition and with the goodness condition, and these data form a witness at the level of the core; movement of witnesses
transports it to the datum and object of the ledger part of the bundle point, with the entry condition. Zeta compatibility holds at all
weighted points and all contexts by \cref{pf:bundles:lem:zeta}, a fortiori eventually. The bundle statement is \cref{pf:bundles:lem:bwit}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Unlocalised saturated states}\label{pf:bundles:saturated}

\paragraph{Real data, pre-data and states.}
For a column witness $w$, a level $Z$ and a set $W$ of places of the coefficient field, a \emph{real datum} $z$ at $(Z,W)$ is the record
of \cref{sec:families:theta} (realisation of the theta data at $Z$, with its face and geometry, the twisted characters being those at the
places of $W$) together with a character core and pins. It is \emph{fine} if it satisfies the real-data condition (existence of the
archimedean intertwiners and lifts), the geometry condition, the core condition (the laws of its core, canonicity of its parameters,
non-degeneracy of the signatures and agreement of its pre-base data with the frame) and standardness. Let $W_1^0(z)$ be the set of places
$v$ of $\mathit{LL}$ such that, at $v$ or at $\bar v$, one of the lattice indices of $z$ is non-zero (for some row $j$: the index of the
lattice of the division datum relative to the image of the type lattice $T_j$, or the index of $T_j$ relative to its dual for the hermitian
form $H_j$); let $W_1^{\mathrm f}$ be the set of places $v$ such that the forced content $\Phi_\bundK(j,\ell_v)$ of the scale of $\bundK$ is
non-zero for some $j$ (it depends only on $\ell_v=\ell_{\bar v}$; we write $\Phi_\bundK$ also for the finite set of primes $\ell$ with
$\Phi_\bundK(j,\ell)\neq0$ for some $j$); and put $W_1(z)=W_1^0(z)\cup W_1^{\mathrm f}$.

For a level $Z$ of $w$ we use: the \emph{group conditions} (the types of the norm stage are the true types of the frame, and the group
conditions hold at the stage tuple); the \emph{$\kappa$-condition} (the $\kappa$-values of the datum are the numbers $a^{\pm}$ of its
Harish-Chandra parameters); the \emph{volume tie} (the automorphic quotients have the volumes prescribed by the datum); and the
\emph{volume condition} (every level with the volume--jet stage of $Z$ satisfies the volume tie and the numerical condition of the scale
of $\bundK$, and admits column data at split primes).

We write $B\preceq_0B'$ if $B$ and $B'$ lie over the same pre-base, the images of $K_s$ agree, $\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}\subseteq
\mathit{LL}'_{0,\C}$ and $\mathit{LL}_\C\subseteq\mathit{LL}'_\C$; then a place $v'$ of $\mathit{LL}'$ \emph{lies over} a place $v$ of
$\mathit{LL}$ if $v'$ restricts to $v$ under the embedding $\mathit{LL}\hookrightarrow\mathit{LL}'$ compatible with the embeddings into
$\C$. For real data $z$ over $B$ and $z'$ over $B'$, $z'$ \emph{extends $z$ except for the places} if the cores of $z$ and $z'$ coincide
and their real data agree component by component (characters, Weil representations and theta data, lattices and frames, measures, forms,
reference vectors and jets, values in the coefficient fields being compared in $\C$); $z'$ \emph{extends} $z$ if moreover $v\in W_1^0(z)$
if and only if $v'\in W_1^0(z')$, whenever $v'$ lies over $v$.

A \emph{pre-datum} $\mathcal P$ over $w$ consists of
a level $Z_0$, a set $W_t$ of places, a real datum $z$ at $(Z_0,W_t)$ with a row package, together with the conditions: the volume and
group conditions at $Z_0$, coherence of $(B,T)$, the $\kappa$-condition, the primitive row conditions for $z$, the real-data and geometry
conditions, the core condition, $W_t=W_1(z)$, every place of $W_t$ has residue characteristic in $T.\mathrm{pl}$, $\psi_v=1$ for
$v\notin W_t$, and standardness of $z$. A \emph{state} $x$ of $\mathcal P$ consists of stages $(V,S,N,G)$ over the row package of
$\mathcal P$ (division stage, $S$-stage, norm stage and volume--jet stage) which \emph{fit} the row package, with $G$ the stage of $Z_0$,
the division factor $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$, the law of the resulting datum, and a moved copy with coherence, a side, a reading and a pin.
The state is \emph{unlocalised} if its norm stage has no localisation primes and all its $\eta$-components $\eta_j(v)$ vanish. The
pre-datum is \emph{saturated} if, when $T$ is untwisted, any two places of $\mathit{LL}$ above the same place of $\mathit{LL}_0$ lie
both in $W_t$ or both outside $W_t$.

\begin{definition}\label{pf:bundles:def:US}
\emph{Unlocalised saturated states exist}, $(\mathrm{US})$, if $\mathrm{Step}_W$ holds from starting witnesses to: there are a
saturated pre-datum $\mathcal P$ over $w$ and an unlocalised state of $\mathcal P$.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{The inputs.}
The proof uses four conditions on real data. In the first two, $z$ ranges over real data at levels $Z$ of $w$ satisfying the group, real-data
and geometry conditions (and, for the first, the further conditions in the statement of $\sfname{RowRat}$).
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\textsc{\roman*})]
\item \emph{Weight case} (WC): $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from starting witnesses to: the primitive row conditions hold for $z$.
\item \emph{Lower unitary case} (LU): $\mathrm{Step}$ to: for every pinned witness $w$, the lift conditions hold for $z$ (Hecke rationality and
adjoint-closedness of the rows and lifts at inert places, the Hecke condition, the unit and equivariance conditions, the block condition, two lift
tests, surjectivity of the lifts, uniformity, the modular lattice condition, the level condition, the scalar condition at the type parts (the lift
composed with its adjoint acts on each type part by a scalar), the parts condition and type separation).
\item \emph{Fine adelic data} (FA): $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses to: for every level $Z$ satisfying the group conditions and
the volume tie there is a fine real datum at $(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$.
\item \emph{Extension to enlarged bases} (FE): $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses $w'$ over $(B',f',T')$ to: for every base $B$ with
$B\preceq_0B'$, the frame $f=f'$, every twisting datum $T$ over $B$, every pinned witness $w$ for $(B,f,T)$ with the same prime data as $w'$
(the generators of the auxiliary primes and the bounds fixed by a column witness), every level $Z$ of $w$ satisfying the group conditions,
every fine real datum $z$ at $(Z,W)$ (for any $W$) satisfying the lift conditions, and every level $Z'$ of $w'$ satisfying the group conditions
and the volume tie, there is a fine real datum $z'$ at $(Z',\{v':\ell_{v'}\in T'.\mathrm{pl}\})$ which extends $z$.
\end{enumerate}
(LU) follows from $\mathcal H_\Theta$, the continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions and (C1)
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.lu_of_multiplicity_equal}: the lift conditions are proved at a bare level with the same group data, from the ties of
the real datum and equal multiplicities, and transported to the given level; the proof at the bare level uses the scalar identity and the matching
of the lifts, which rest on the adjoint scalar formula \cref{pf:bundles:cor:bform} with the Rallis inner product formula supplied by $\mathcal
H_\Theta$). The other three conditions are proved in the following lemmas.

\begin{lemma}[Fine real data]\label{pf:bundles:lem:FA}
The discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, rapid decay, Casselman--Wallach, Weil representations of unitary pairs, existence of
discrete series and Harish-Chandra finiteness imply (FA).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.exists_fineAdelicDatum_of_hypotheses}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Three statements hold eventually and for large twisting data: the host hypotheses at all stage tuples over the pre-base, including the
diagonal positive definite forms with standard lattices and orthonormal frames of the definite spaces
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.HostFrames.hostHyp_frames_ev}, which proves this eventual statement); the standardness and largeness of the
definite spaces (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.eventually_definiteSpaces_standard_and_large}); and, for column inputs whose characters range
over $\Xi(T,f)$, the containment of the character values in the coefficient field together with the admissibility of the type families
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.valsIn_and_fam3_ok_of_coefficientField}). Fix $\bundK,B,f,T$ at which they hold, a pinned witness $w$ and a level
$Z$ satisfying the group conditions and the volume tie, and let $W=\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\}$. We construct a fine real datum at $(Z,W)$.

The column pin selects one place in each pair of conjugate places over the twisting primes
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.Rw.selHalf_of_pin}); the set $W$ is finite (finitely many places of $\mathit{LL}$ lie over the finite set
$T.\mathrm{pl}$) and stable under conjugation. Since $K_s$ is exact for $f$, the character field of the frame lies in $K_{s,\C}$, and by the
valuation conditions of $w$ the values of the frame characters lie in $\mathit{LL}_0$. From this and the volume tie one obtains a \emph{core pin}: a character core $\gamma_0$ whose
coefficient field embeds into $\mathit{LL}$ compatibly with the embeddings into $\C$, with values in $\mathit{LL}_0$ and image containing
$K_s$ and $E$, satisfying the core laws and pinned to the pre-base data of $f$, such that the character values at ideles with trivial
infinity part lie in its coefficient field (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.character_value_mem_smallField_of_infPart_eq_one}, which constructs it).
Next, the analytic data at the column choices exist: characters $X_g$, a trace datum, a first real datum $R_0$ and a group identification,
with the clauses of the column choices (level data, identities, good characters, the $\mu$-condition, the idempotent condition, the
pre-condition, and the input, tuple and keeping clauses) (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_analyticData_kept_clauses_columnChoices}). For
real data with these clauses the archimedean intertwiners and lifts exist, by the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, rapid decay and
Casselman--Wallach (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.intertwinersAndLifts_exist_at_choices}); and rapid decay gives the geometric existence of the
core forms (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.geometricExistence_core_forms_analytic_phaseOne}, which constructs the forms and frames of a geometry
from the analytic data and the core pin). Weil representations, existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra finiteness and rapid decay
supply the analytic links of the realisation (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.linksAnalyticHypotheses_of_weilRep_harishChandra}), and with them
the standard weight-character construction (\lref{GlobalTheory.StandardWeightCharacter.charW_seed_std}, which completes the analytic
data to a real datum) gives a trace datum, a dictionary, characters $\mu$, $\chi_W$ and $c_E$, a base point $x_0$, a real datum $R$ with the
level data of $R_0$, pins, the real-data condition and a geometry. Let $z$ be the real datum at $(Z,W)$ with these components, whose core is
$\gamma_0$ enlarged by the dictionary, $\mu$, $\chi_W$, $x_0$ and $c_E$.

The datum $z$ is fine: the real-data condition holds by construction; the core laws, canonicity, the signature condition and the pre-base
condition follow from the core pin and the laws of the geometry; standardness is provided by the construction; and the geometry condition
follows from rapid decay and the pre-character conditions of $R$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.RealGeoIn.inGeo_and_lUnif_and_lIntegral}, which
proves the geometry condition together with the uniformity and integrality of the lifts).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Extension to enlarged bases]\label{pf:bundles:lem:FE}
The discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, rapid decay, Casselman--Wallach, Weil representations of unitary pairs, existence of
discrete series and Harish-Chandra finiteness imply (FE).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.adelicData_extend_largerFields_of_hypotheses}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We prove two statements of the form $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses $w'$, with the data $B\preceq_0B'$, $f$, $T$, $w$, $Z$, $z$, $Z'$
as in (FE); together they give (FE).

(i) \emph{There is a fine real datum $z'$ at $(Z',\{v':\ell_{v'}\in T'.\mathrm{pl}\})$ which extends $z$ except for the places.} First the core
of $z$ is transported to the enlarged base: there is a character core $\gamma'$ over the stage tuple of $Z'$ whose coefficient field embeds
into $\mathit{LL}'$ compatibly with the embeddings into $\C$, with values in $\mathit{LL}'_0$ and image containing $K'_s$, whose components
coincide with those of the core of $z$, and which satisfies the core laws, canonicity, the signature condition and the pre-base condition
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_adelicCore_transport_enlargedCoefficientBase_alt}, which deduces this from $B\preceq_0B'$, $f'=f$ and the core
condition of $z$). The seven classical inputs then give a real datum $z'$ at $(Z',\{v':\ell_{v'}\in T'.\mathrm{pl}\})$ with core $\gamma'$,
satisfying the real-data, geometry and standardness conditions, which extends $z$ except for the places
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_extendedAdelicData_with_intertwiners_over_enlargedCore}, which rebuilds the analytic data, the characterised Weil
data and the geometric half of $z$ over the enlarged coefficient field with the same values). The core condition of $z'$ is that of $\gamma'$, so
$z'$ is fine.

(ii) \emph{If $z'$ is a fine real datum at $(Z',\{v':\ell_{v'}\in T'.\mathrm{pl}\})$ which extends $z$ except for the places, then $v\in W_1^0(z)$
if and only if $v'\in W_1^0(z')$ whenever $v'$ lies over $v$.} Let $\varphi\colon\mathit{LL}\to\mathit{LL}'$ be the embedding compatible with the
embeddings into $\C$. The lattices whose indices define $W_1^0$ (for each row $j$, the lattice of the division datum and the image of the type
lattice $T_j$; and $T_j$ and its dual for $H_j$) are, for $z'$, the base changes along $\varphi$ of those for $z$: for the lattice of lifted
forms and for the definite lift lattice this holds for every extension of fine real data satisfying the lift conditions
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.liftFormsLattice_definiteLattice_baseChange_extended} and \lref{GlobalTheory.T.extendedData_definiteLiftLattice_baseChange},
which prove these two base change statements from the seven classical inputs), and the hermitian dual of a base change is the base change of the
hermitian dual (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.hermitianDualLattice_baseChange}). For finitely generated lattices over $\OO_{\mathit{LL}}$ and a place
$v'$ over $v$, the index of one lattice relative to another is non-zero at $v$ if and only if the index of their base changes is non-zero at $v'$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.lattice_index_nonzero_iff_under_field_map}). Hence each defining index of $W_1^0(z)$ at $v$ is non-zero exactly
when the corresponding index of $z'$ at $v'$ is non-zero; the same holds for $\bar v$ and $\bar v'$, and the assertion follows.

By (i) and (ii), the datum $z'$ of (i) extends $z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The weight case]\label{pf:bundles:lem:WC}
$\mathcal H_\Theta$, the continuation of partial Hecke $L$-functions and (C2) imply (WC).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.w_of_weight_residue_char}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Starting witnesses are pinned. By (C2) it therefore suffices to prove $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses to: for every level $Z$ with the
group conditions and every real datum $z$ at $Z$ satisfying the real-data and geometry conditions, $\mathrm{EM}(z.R)$ and $\sfname{RowTest}(z)$
imply the primitive row conditions for $z$. We use the following statements, each of which holds eventually and for large twisting data:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the \emph{sized criterion for primitive rows}: for every $\eps>0$ there is $x_0$ such that for every stage tuple, every real datum $z$
and every size datum $L$ of scale $(\eps,x)$ with $x\ge x_0$, satisfying the size conditions and admitting a parameter choice, the base and
geometric ties of $z$, $\mathrm{EM}(z.R)$, $\sfname{RowTest}(z)$ and the integrality of the lifts imply the primitive row conditions
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.rational_data_sized_family_alt}, derived from $\mathcal H_\Theta$ and the continuation of partial Hecke
$L$-functions); eventually it applies with $\eps=\eps(\bundK)$ and $x_0\le x(\bundK)$;
\item size data with the ledger, size and parameter conditions and with the scale of $\bundK$ exist at every stage tuple over $\bundK$ with
division factor $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.LedRec.ev_exists_sizeData});
\item the host hypotheses (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.HostFrames.hostHyp_frames_ev}, recalled in the proof of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:FA});
\item the coefficient-field statement of the proof of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:FA}
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.valsIn_and_fam3_ok_of_coefficientField}), and the Weber level condition, which holds for large twisting data
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.CoverAt.lg_weberLev});
\item at pinned witnesses, every real datum with the real-data and geometry conditions has uniform and integral lifts
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.lUnif_atDat} and \lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.atDat_lIntegral}, which read these properties off the
geometry of the datum).
\end{enumerate}
Fix data at which all of them hold, a pinned witness $w$, a level $Z$ with the group conditions, and $z$ with the real-data and geometry
conditions, $\mathrm{EM}(z.R)$ and $\sfname{RowTest}(z)$. Green's cuspidal characters (a component of $\mathcal H_\Theta$) give a bare level
$Z'$ of $w$ with the same group data as $Z$, true types and division factor $6x^{-C_F}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.LedRec.exists_bare}), and
(ii) gives size data $L$ at
the stage tuple of $Z'$ with the scale of $\bundK$. Let $y$ be the copy of $z$ at $Z'$. Since $Z$ and $Z'$ have the same group data, the
real-data condition, the uniformity and integrality of the lifts, $\mathrm{EM}$ and $\sfname{RowTest}$ pass from $z$ to $y$, and the primitive
row conditions for $y$ imply those for $z$. For $y$: the types are true; the orthonormal frames of (iii) and the characterisation contained in
the pins give the extension property of the archimedean data (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.ioExt_of_weakCharacterisation_of_orthonormal_frames});
with the phases of the geometry this gives the one-form map rows at the three levels
(\lref{GlobalTheory.OneMapRow3.oneFormMapRow_threeLevel_of_characterisation}); together with the uniformity of the lifts and the cover field
of the Weber level condition these form the geometric ties of $y$. The base ties of $y$ consist of the coefficient-field statement (iv), the
pins, the real-data condition, the host hypotheses, the parameter choice of $L$, the class group condition of the base, the guard, good types and
the series clause (both from the true types; \lref{GlobalTheory.LiftRows.Inh.goodTypes_of_typesTrue}), the inert clause, and the trace,
standardness and phase conditions of the pins. Hence (i) applies to $y$ and $L$: the primitive row conditions hold for $y$, hence for $z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Saturation over the fixed field]\label{pf:bundles:lem:sat}
Green's cuspidal characters, (FA), (LU) and (FE) imply $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses to: if $T$ is untwisted, there are a level $Z$
and a fine real datum $z$ at $(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$ such that $Z$ satisfies the volume, group and $\kappa$-conditions, all places
of $W_1(z)$ have residue characteristic in $T.\mathrm{pl}$, $\psi_v=1$ for $v\notin W_1(z)$, and $W_1(z)$ is a union of fibres of the places of
$\mathit{LL}$ over those of $\mathit{LL}_0$: if $v\in W_1(z)$ and $v'$ lies over the same place of $\mathit{LL}_0$ as $v$, then $v'\in W_1(z)$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.untwisted_adelic_data_saturated_over_fixed_field}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Seeds.} For a context $\bundK$, a pre-base $P$, a frame $f$, subfields $F,F_0,L$ of $\C$, prime data $\pi$ and a set $S$ of primes, a
\emph{seed} consists of a base $B_0$ over $P$ with $K_{s,\C}=F$ and $\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}=F_0$, a twisting datum $T_0$, a pinned witness $w_0$
for $(B_0,f,T_0)$ with prime data $\pi$, a level $Z_0$ of $w_0$ with the group conditions, and a fine real datum $z_0$ at some $(Z_0,W_0)$
satisfying the lift conditions, such that all places of $W_1^0(z_0)$ have residue characteristic in $S$ and $\mathit{LL}_\C(B_0)\subseteq L$.
Seeds are monotone in $S$ and $L$. For all $\bundK,P,f,F$ there are a finite set $S(P,f,F)$ of primes and a finite set $S_2(P,f,F)$ of
algebraic numbers such that for every $\pi$: if a seed exists for some $F_0$, with $S$ the set of all primes and $L=\C$, then one exists for
some $F_0$ with $S=S(P,f,F)$ and $L=\Q(S_2(P,f,F))$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.exists_uniform_normalisedAdelicSeeds}, the uniform
normalisation of seeds). We use these sets as exceptional sets in the definition of large twisting data (\cref{pf:bundles:def:large}): the
prime set $S(P,f,K_{s,\C})\cup\Phi_\bundK$ and the set $S_2(P,f,K_{s,\C})$ of algebraic numbers. Hence, for large twisting data,
$\Phi_\bundK\subseteq T.\mathrm{pl}$ and, by monotonicity, for every $\pi$: if a seed with $F=K_{s,\C}$ exists, then one exists with
$F=K_{s,\C}$, $S=T.\mathrm{pl}$ and $L=\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}$ (the \emph{seed condition}).

\emph{Construction.} The conditions (FA), (LU), (FE), the seed condition and the existence of a true volume stage hold simultaneously; the
last is the statement $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses to: there is a volume--jet stage $G$ over the columns of $w$ such that every
level with volume--jet stage $G$ satisfies the volume condition (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.TrueLocal.exists_trueVolumeStage_columnChoiceStep}).
Let $w$ be a pinned witness for $(B,f,T)$ at such data, with $T$ untwisted.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item Green's cuspidal characters give a level $Z$ with volume--jet stage $G$ satisfying the group and $\kappa$-conditions
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.Rw.exists_lt_grpTrue}, which constructs a level with true group data over any volume--jet stage); it
satisfies the volume condition, hence the volume tie.
\item (FA) gives a fine real datum $z_1$ at $(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$, and (LU) the lift conditions for $z_1$. Thus $(B,T,w,Z,z_1)$
is a seed with $F=K_{s,\C}$, $F_0=\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}$, all primes as $S$ and $L=\C$.
\item By the seed condition there is a seed $(B_0,T_0,w_0,Z_0,z_0)$ with $K_{s,\C}(B_0)=K_{s,\C}(B)$, the prime data of $w$, residue
characteristics of $W_1^0(z_0)$ in $T.\mathrm{pl}$ and $\mathit{LL}_\C(B_0)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}(B)$. Then $B_0\preceq_0B$: the pre-bases
and the images of $K_s$ agree, and $\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}(B_0)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_\C(B_0)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}(B)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_\C(B)$.
\item (FE), applied to $w'=w$, $Z'=Z$ and the seed, gives a fine real datum $z$ at $(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$ which extends $z_0$.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Verification.} $Z$ satisfies the volume, group and $\kappa$-conditions, and $z$ is fine. As $\mathit{LL}_\C(B_0)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_\C(B)$,
every place $v$ of $\mathit{LL}$ lies over a unique place $v_0$ of the coefficient field of $B_0$, and $\ell_v=\ell_{v_0}$. Let $v\in W_1(z)$.
If $v\in W_1^0(z)$, then $v_0\in W_1^0(z_0)$ because $z$ extends $z_0$, so $\ell_v=\ell_{v_0}\in T.\mathrm{pl}$; if $v\in W_1^{\mathrm f}$, then
$\ell_v\in\Phi_\bundK\subseteq T.\mathrm{pl}$. Since $T$ is untwisted, $\psi_v=1$ for all $v$. For saturation let $v\in W_1(z)$, and let $v'$ lie
over the same place of $\mathit{LL}_0$ as $v$. If $v\in W_1^0(z)$, then $v_0\in W_1^0(z_0)$; since the coefficient field of $B_0$ embeds into
$\mathit{LL}_0$ (as $\mathit{LL}_\C(B_0)\subseteq\mathit{LL}_{0,\C}(B)$), the restrictions of $v$ and $v'$ to it are the restrictions of their
common restriction to $\mathit{LL}_0$, so $v'_0=v_0$, and $v'\in W_1^0(z)$ because $z$ extends $z_0$. If $v\in W_1^{\mathrm f}$, then
$v'\in W_1^{\mathrm f}$, since $\ell_{v'}=\ell_v$ and membership in $W_1^{\mathrm f}$ depends only on the residue characteristic.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Starting choices]\label{pf:bundles:lem:start}
Weil representations of unitary pairs, existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra finiteness, rapid decay and (WC) imply $\mathrm{Step}_W$
from starting witnesses to: for every level $Z$ satisfying the volume, group and $\kappa$-conditions and every fine real datum $z$ at
$(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$ such that all places of $W_1(z)$ have residue characteristic in $T.\mathrm{pl}$ and $\psi_v=1$ for
$v\notin W_1(z)$, there is a pre-datum $\mathcal P$ with $Z_0=Z$ and $W_t=W_1(z)$ whose real datum extends $z$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.constructionStartingChoices_of_fine_adelicData}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Weil representations, existence of discrete series, Harish-Chandra finiteness and rapid decay give the analytic inputs of the row
constructions (\lref{GlobalTheory.LinksInputs.inputs_of_hypotheses}, which assembles them). We may work at a starting witness $w$ at which
(WC) and the host hypotheses hold (the latter hold eventually, \lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.HostFrames.hostHyp_frames_ev}). Let $Z$ and $z$ be as
in the statement and $W_t=W_1(z)$; then $W_t\subseteq\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\}$, and $W_t$ is stable under conjugation, since the conditions
defining $W_1$ are symmetric in $v$ and $\bar v$.

\emph{Restriction.} Let $z'$ be the restriction of $z$ to $W_t$: the real datum at $(Z,W_t)$ with the same core, analytic data and geometry, in
which the twisted characters are kept at the places of $W_t$ only (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.ResStd.res}, which constructs it for a
conjugation-stable subset of the twisted places). The lattice indices defining $W_1$ are unchanged by the restriction, so $W_1(z')=W_1(z)$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.ResStd.restrictAdelicDataTwistedPlaces_nonzeroIndexPlaces}); the real-data, geometry, core and standardness
conditions pass to $z'$, so $z'$ is fine; and $z'$ extends $z$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.ResStd.res_ext}).

\emph{Primitive rows.} The datum $z'$ satisfies the hypotheses of (WC): the volume, group, $\kappa$-, real-data, geometry and core conditions,
$W_t=W_1(z')$, and the residue characteristics of $W_t$ lie in $T.\mathrm{pl}$. Hence the primitive row conditions hold for $z'$.

\emph{Row package.} The host hypotheses give the maximal frame and the stage conditions of the stage tuple of $Z$. From these, the analytic inputs,
the primitive row conditions and the conjugation stability of $W_1(z')$ one obtains a row package for $z'$, i.e.\ a family of row lifts over the
column choices (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.nonempty_rowLiftFamily_over_columnChoices}, which constructs the row lifts of a real datum with primitive
rows).

\emph{Assembly.} The level $Z$, the set $W_t$, the datum $z'$ with its row package and the conditions --- the volume condition, coherence of $(B,T)$
(part of being starting), the group and $\kappa$-conditions, the primitive row conditions, the real-data, geometry and core conditions,
$W_t=W_1(z')$, the residue characteristics, $\psi_v=1$ off $W_t$ (a hypothesis) and standardness --- form a pre-datum $\mathcal P$ with $Z_0=Z$
and $W_t=W_1(z)$, whose real datum $z'$ extends $z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[$S$-stages with the true Haar data]\label{pf:bundles:lem:sstage}
Let $\bundK$ be a context over the pre-base of $B$, $C$ columns over $B$ with positive column data, $W$ a row rest over $C$ whose number of rows
is at most its total dimension, and $c$ a Haar datum. There is an $S$-stage $S$ over $(B,C,W)$ whose gap parameter is $\Delta(\bundK)$, whose
column scale is the true scale of $c$ and whose archimedean densities are the true densities of the Harish-Chandra parameters of $B$, such
that for all division, norm and volume--jet stages $V,N,G$ and every prime $\mathfrak p$ of $F$ the datum assembled from $(B,C,W,V,S,N,G)$ is
locally isomorphic at $\mathfrak p$ to its local model.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.Mem.SProv.exists_sStage_true}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $\Delta=\Delta(\bundK)>0$. Since $\bundK$ lies over the pre-base, its code is an injective map $\mathrm{cd}_\bundK$ from the words of the
pre-base to binary strings of length $k-2$ such that, for every word $\omega$ and infinite place $\tau$, the Harish-Chandra parameters
$(u_l,v_l)$ of $\omega$ at $\tau$ satisfy $v_l-v_{l+1}=4\Delta$ and $u_{l+1}=v_l-\frac12-\Delta\,\mathrm{cd}_\bundK(\omega)_l$; this follows from
the formula for the parameters of the words over a context (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.SProv.code_of_over}). The unconditional construction of $S$-stages
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.SpectralModel.exists_sStage}: for a code of the words with these relations, positive column data, a row rest with at
most as many rows as its total dimension and any positive archimedean densities there is an $S$-stage with these densities, built from the inner
products of the column spaces and an isometry of the row space onto a standard space) applies with the code of $\bundK$ and the true densities,
which are positive. In the resulting $S$-stage we replace the column scale by the true scale of $c$, the gap parameter by $\Delta$, and the local
datum at each prime $\mathfrak p$ of $F$ by the standard local datum of $E/F$ at $\mathfrak p$ together with split data, which we choose to exist
exactly when the residue characteristic of $\mathfrak p$ is one of the auxiliary primes $g_0,g_1$: these primes split in $E$, so that there are
two primes of $E$ above $\mathfrak p$, and split data exist (\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.SProv.exists_split}). The relations between code,
gap parameter and parameters are those of $\Delta$, so this is again an $S$-stage. Finally, for all $V,N,G$ and $\mathfrak p$ the local datum of
the assembled datum at $\mathfrak p$ is the standard local datum with the chosen split data, which is locally isomorphic to the local model
(\lref{Formalism.L2.S.loc_of_adj}, which constructs the isomorphism).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{pf:bundles:prop:US}
$\mathcal H_A$ and $\mathcal H_C$ imply $(\mathrm{US})$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.compatibleSystem_weightOne_saturated}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The classical inputs of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:FA,pf:bundles:lem:FE,pf:bundles:lem:WC,pf:bundles:lem:sat,pf:bundles:lem:start} and of (LU) are
components of $\mathcal H_A$ and $\mathcal H_C$, so (WC), (LU), (FA) and (FE) hold, and with them the conclusion (a) of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:sat}
and the conclusion (b) of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:start}. We also use (c), \emph{localisation-free norm stages}
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.localizationFreeStage_trueGroup_columnChoiceStep}, which states the following):
$\mathrm{Step}_W$ from pinned witnesses admitting a pre-datum to: for every pre-datum $\mathcal P$ there is a norm stage over its row
package without localisation primes, with true group data, such that the group conditions hold at every stage tuple built from it and
the centre condition holds for every standard isomorphism.

\emph{Step 1: saturated pre-data.} We show $\mathrm{Step}_W$ from starting witnesses to ``$w$ is pinned and there is a saturated
pre-datum''. Starting witnesses are pinned, so (a) and (b) apply. Let $w$ be a starting witness. If $T$ is untwisted, (a) gives $Z$ and $z$,
and (b) gives a pre-datum $\mathcal P$ with $Z_0=Z$ and $W_t=W_1(z)$; since $W_1(z)$ is a union of fibres, $\mathcal P$ is saturated. If
$T$ is not untwisted, then, $w$ being starting, the datum of $w$ is good at some level: there are a level $Z$ and a real datum $z$ at
$(Z,\{v:\ell_v\in T.\mathrm{pl}\})$ satisfying the volume, group, $\kappa$-, real-data and geometry conditions, the core condition and
standardness (so $z$ is fine) and the conditions on $W_1(z)$; (b) gives a pre-datum, which is saturated because the premise ``$T$
untwisted'' of saturation fails.

\emph{Step 2: norm stages.} A pinned witness with a pre-datum satisfies the hypothesis of (c); composing with Step~1 gives
$\mathrm{Step}_W$ from starting witnesses to: there is a saturated pre-datum $\mathcal P$, and for every pre-datum there is a norm stage
$N$ as in (c).

\emph{Step 3: states.} Let $\mathcal P$ be saturated and $N$ the norm stage of (c) for $\mathcal P$. There is a division stage $V$ over the
row package with prescribed $\eta$-components $\eta=0$, division factor $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$ and a division lattice given by the level-$0$
lattices of the columns, such that the Bessel values of $V$ are rational whenever they lie in the image of $\mathit{LL}$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.R3.Rw.exists_divStage_of_pk}, which constructs division stages with prescribed data), and there is an
$S$-stage over the rows with the true Haar data of the volume--jet stage of $Z_0$ (\cref{pf:bundles:lem:sstage}; the column data of a
pinned witness are positive, and the row package has at most as many rows as its total dimension). The
stages $(V,S,N,G)$, with $G$ the stage of $Z_0$, fit the row package (the division, group and centre conditions come from the choices of $V$
and $N$), have $\eta_F=6x^{-C_F}$, and the law of the datum holds by the rationality property of $V$. Hence they form a state $x$ of
$\mathcal P$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.fittingLaterStages_row_is_constructionState}: fitting stages with these properties are the
underlying stages of a state). The norm stage of $x$ is $N$, which has no localisation primes, and its $\eta$-components are those of $V$,
which vanish. This proves $(\mathrm{US})$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Inner products along twisted theta chains}\label{pf:bundles:chains}

This subsection proves the inner product formula along the chain of theta lifts of a datum, and its consequence for the composite of the
chain lift with its adjoint. Both are used in the proof of the lower unitary case (LU) of \cref{pf:bundles:saturated}.

\subsubsection{Parameter families and partial $L$-functions}

A \emph{parameter family} $B$ over $E$ assigns to each finite place $w$ of $E$ a finite multiset $B_w$ of non-zero complex numbers. For a
set $S$ of finite places of $F$ let $S_E$ be the set of places of $E$ above $S$; put
\[
L^S(B,s)=\prod_{w\notin S_E}\ \prod_{b\in B_w}\bigl(1-b\,\Nm w^{-s}\bigr)^{-1}
\]
(an unconditional product; it is a convergent Euler product at $s$ if no factor vanishes and $\sum_w|\prod_b(1-b\Nm w^{-s})-1|<\infty$).
$B$ is \emph{bounded with exponent $\kappa$ outside $S$} (with respect to $r$) if $|B_w|\le r$ and $|b|\le\Nm w^{\kappa}$ for $b\in B_w$,
$w\notin S_E$. For a unitary Hecke character $\chi$ of $E$ write $\chi(w)$ for its value at a uniformiser of $E_w$ and
$L^S(B\otimes\chi^{-1},s)$ for $L^S$ of the family $w\mapsto\chi(w)^{-1}B_w$. The \emph{segment} of length $t$ at $w$ with centre $z$ is
$\mathrm{seg}_w(z,t)=\{z\Nm w^{(t-1)/2-i}:0\le i<t\}$ (\cref{def:families:string}). A function $h$ has \emph{holomorphic value} $c$ at
$s_0$ if there is a function $H$ which agrees with $h$ on a right half-plane, is meromorphic on a connected open set containing that
half-plane and $s_0$, is analytic at $s_0$, and satisfies $H(s_0)=c$. Finally, for $a,b\in\N$ and a place $\mathfrak P$ put
$\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(a,b)=\prod_{a<n\le b}(1-\Nm\mathfrak P^{-n})$, and let $d^S_{a,b}(s)$ be the partial normalising factor of the
Siegel Eisenstein series on $\Uni(a,a)$ relevant to the pair of ranks $(a,b)$, a product over the places $v\notin S$ of products of
local $L$-factors of powers of the quadratic character of $E/F$.

\subsubsection{Chain data, stages and steps}

Fix a global datum $D$; its ranks satisfy $t\ge r+5$ and $m\ge2$, and the spaces $Y_j$ ($0\le j<m$) of the chain have rank $r+jt$.
\begin{itemize}
\item A \emph{chain datum} $\bundc$ consists of archimedean data, an additive character $\psi$, unitary Hecke characters $\mu_j$ ($j\ge1$)
and $\eta$ of $E$, a finite set $P_\bundc$ of finite places of $E$ with level data and characters $\chi_j(\mathfrak P)$ of the residue fields
($\mathfrak P\in P_\bundc$); $S_\bundc$ is the set of places of $F$ below $P_\bundc$. The \emph{splitting characters} of step $j$ are
$(\chi^S_j,\chi^T_j)=(\mu_j\eta^{-1},\mu_j)$ for $j$ odd and $(\mu_j,\mu_j\eta^{-1})$ for $j$ even. Admissibility requires among others
that $\mu_{j+1}$ has infinity type $-e_j(\tau)$ at each infinite place $\tau$, is unramified outside $S_\bundc$, and restricts to
$\chi_{j+1}(\mathfrak P)^{-1}$ at $\mathfrak P\in P_\bundc$.
\item A \emph{twist} $\bundw$ of $\bundc$ is a decomposition $P_\bundc=P_g\sqcup Q$ into untwisted and twisted places, with characters
$\xi_{\mathfrak P}\ne1$ of the residue fields at $\mathfrak P\in Q$ such that $\chi_j(\mathfrak P)=\xi_{\mathfrak P}^{-1}$ there; $S_0$ is the
set of places of $F$ below $P_g$.
\item A \emph{stage} $s$ consists of a hermitian space $V_s$, a discrete-series model $(H_{s,\tau})_\tau$ at the infinite places, a
self-dual lattice, a frame, a level datum, a Haar measure $\nu_s$ on $\Uni(V_s)(\A_F)$ and a fundamental domain $\mathcal F_s$. Its space
$C(s)$ consists of the automorphic maps of the given level and character: linear maps $u$ from the smooth vectors of $\bigotimes_\tau
H_{s,\tau}$ to functions on $\Uni(V_s)(\A_F)$. The Petersson pairing is $\mathrm{pet}_s(f_1,f_2)=\int_{\mathcal F_s}f_1\bar f_2\,d\nu_s$.
A stage is \emph{at step $j$} of $(\bundc,\bundw)$ if $V_s$ has the rank and signature of $Y_j$, the discrete-series parameters of step
$j$, and the level datum of step $j$ with the twisted places $Q$.
\item A \emph{metric} on a stage is a pair $(\langle\,,\rangle_D,\langle\,,\rangle)$: $\langle\,,\rangle_D$ is the hermitian form on the
vectors which is the product of the Hilbert inner products on pure tensors, and $\langle\,,\rangle$ is a positive definite hermitian form
on $C(s)$ satisfying the Schur relation $\mathrm{pet}_s(u(v),u'(v'))=\langle u,u'\rangle\,\langle v,v'\rangle_D$.
\item A \emph{step} $p$ from $s$ to $s'$ consists of a Weil representation $\omega$ of $\Uni(V_s)\times\Uni(V_{s'})$ with the splitting
characters of the step, a step vector, polynomial test functions $\Phi_\phi$ attached to polynomials $\phi$, archimedean intertwiners
$I_\phi$ from the vectors of $s'$ to those of $s$, and the \emph{downward lift} $\iota_p\colon C(s')\to C(s)$, characterised by
\[
\int_{\mathcal F_{s'}}\theta(g,h;\Phi_\phi)\,y(v)(h)\,d\nu_{s'}(h)=\iota_p(y)(I_\phi v)(g).
\]
The \emph{upward lift} of a function $F$ on $\Uni(V_s)(\A_F)$ is $\theta_\phi(F)(h)=\int_{\mathcal F_s}\overline{\theta(g,h;\Phi_\phi)}F(g)\,
d\nu_s(g)$.
\item For $u\in C(s)$, $u$ \emph{has parameters} $B$ if $u\ne0$ and every non-zero component $u(v)$ is a Hecke eigenfunction outside
$S_\bundc$ with Satake parameters $B$. At the twisted places, $u$ \emph{has the twisted local parameters of step $k$} if at each
$\mathfrak P\in Q$ the conjugates of its components are generalised eigenvectors of the parahoric Hecke operators of the charts
$\GL_{r+kt}\to\Uni(V_s)$ at the place below $\mathfrak P$, of type a lift of $\xi_{\mathfrak P}$ and with parameters $q_k(\mathfrak P)$, where
$q_0=\emptyset$ and $q_{i+1}=q_i\cdot\chi^S_{i+1}(\varpi)\chi^T_{i+1}(\varpi)^{-1}+\mathrm{seg}(\chi^S_{i+1}(\varpi),t)$ (see
\cref{pf:bundles:twisted} for the precise definition).
\item The \emph{chain parameters} are $\mathrm{ch}_0(B)=B$ and $\mathrm{ch}_{k+1}(B)=\mathrm{st}_{k+1}(\mathrm{ch}_k(B))$, where
$\mathrm{st}_{j}(B)_w=B_w\,\chi^S_{j}(w)\chi^T_{j}(w)^{-1}+\mathrm{seg}_w(\chi^S_{j}(w),t)$.
\end{itemize}

For the real data $R$ of the datum and a \emph{tuple} $\bundt$ (an index of the twisted characters of $R$), $R$ provides a chain datum
$\bundc_\bundt$ with $P_{\bundc_\bundt}=P_g\cup Q_\bundt$, a twist $\bundw_\bundt$, stages $s_{\bundt,j}$ ($0\le j<m$) on the spaces $Y_j$,
a top stage $s_{\bundt,m}$ on $X'$, and steps $p_{\bundt,k}$ from $s_{\bundt,k}$ to $s_{\bundt,k+1}$. The archimedean intertwiners of the
steps and the discrete-series models do not depend on $\bundt$, and $\mu_{\bundt,j}=\mu_{0,j}\,\xi_\bundt$ for a character $\xi_\bundt$ which does
not depend on $j$, so that the ratios $\mu_{\bundt,i}\mu_{\bundt,j}^{-1}$ do not depend on $\bundt$. Let $S_\bundt$ be the set of places of $F$ below $P_g\cup Q_\bundt$, and $H_X(\bundt)$
the Hilbert space of the top stage, with the map $\Theta_\bundt\colon H_X(\bundt)\to H_E$ to the base Hilbert space of forms on $Y_0$.

\begin{definition}[Lifts and scalars]\label{pf:bundles:def:scalars}
Let $\bundt$ be a tuple and $\lambda$ a system of Hecke eigenvalues at the places of $E$ outside $S_\bundt$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item For automorphic maps $u,u'$ at stage $k$, $\langle u,u'\rangle_k=a$ means: $\int_{\mathcal F_k}\overline{u(v)}\,u'(v')\,d\nu_k=a\prod_\tau
\langle v_\tau,v'_\tau\rangle$ for all pure tensors $v,v'$.
\item The \emph{row part} $E_\lambda(\bundt)$ is the space of base-level maps of the type of the datum whose components are eigenfunctions
with eigenvalues $\lambda$ outside $S_\bundt$.
\item $x$ is a \emph{lift} of $u$ at step $k$ ($k+1<m$) if $x\in C(s_{\bundt,k+1})$ and $\langle x,y\rangle_{k+1}=\langle
u,\iota_{p_{\bundt,k}}(y)\rangle_k$ for all $y\in C(s_{\bundt,k+1})$; a \emph{top lift} of $u$ at stage $m-1$ is $x\in H_X(\bundt)$ with
$\langle u,\iota(y)\rangle_{m-1}=\langle x,y\rangle$ for all $y\in H_X(\bundt)$, $\iota$ the downward lift of the top step.
\item The \emph{reached} vectors: $\mathcal R_0=E_\lambda(\bundt)$ and $\mathcal R_{k+1}=\{$lifts of elements of $\mathcal R_k\}$.
\item $L$-values: $l$ is the $L$-value of $(\bundt,B,j)$ if $L^{S_\bundt}(B\otimes\mu_{\bundt,j}^{-1},s)$ is a convergent Euler product at
$s=(1+t)/2$, $l>0$, and $L^{S_\bundt}(B\otimes\mu_{\bundt,j}^{-1},(1+t)/2)=l$.
\item The datum has \emph{chain scalars} if there are $\kappa_1,\dots,\kappa_m>0$ such that for every tuple $\bundt$ and every $\lambda$ with
$E_\lambda(\bundt)\ne0$: there is a family $B$ of Satake parameters of length $r$ of $\lambda$ outside $S_\bundt$; and for every such $B$, (i)
for every $k$ with $k+1<m$ there is an $L$-value $l$ of $(\bundt,B,k+1)$ such that every $u\in\mathcal R_k$ has a lift and $\langle
x_1,x_2\rangle_{k+1}=\kappa_{k+1}l\,\langle u_1,u_2\rangle_k$ for all $u_1,u_2\in\mathcal R_k$ and lifts $x_1,x_2$; (ii) there is an
$L$-value $l$ of $(\bundt,B,m)$ such that every $u\in\mathcal R_{m-1}$ has a top lift and $\langle x_1,x_2\rangle=\kappa_m l\,\langle
u_1,u_2\rangle_{m-1}$ for top lifts $x_i$ of $u_i\in\mathcal R_{m-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Rallis inner product formula for one step]\label{pf:bundles:def:rallis}
The datum $D$ satisfies the \emph{Rallis inner product formula} if the following holds for every pair $(V,V')$ of hermitian spaces with a
Weil representation $\omega$ of $\Uni(V)\times\Uni(V')$ with splitting characters $(\chi_V,\chi_{V'})$, $\rank V'=\rank V+t$, $t\ge2$, and the Witt
index of $V'$ zero or less than $t$; self-dual lattices, a finite set $S$ of places outside of which all data are unramified, Fock test
vectors $\Phi_1,\Phi_2$, Haar measures $m,m'$, cusp forms $u_1,u_2$ on $\Uni(V)$ with $u_1$ of Satake parameters $B$ outside $S$ and $u_2$
spherical outside $S$, a split place $q\notin S$ at which $\beta\chi_{V'}(w)^{-1}\neq\Nm w^{k/2}$ for all $\beta\in B_w$, $w\mid q$ and
$k\in\{\pm(t-1),\pm(t+1)\}$, and integrability of the doubling integrand at some $s_1<t/2$: the lifts $\theta_{\Phi_i}(u_i)$ converge, the
product of the Weil coefficient $\langle\omega(g)\Phi_1,\Phi_2\rangle$ and the matrix coefficient of $(u_1,u_2)$ is integrable on the set
$\mathcal O_S$ of elements integral outside $S$, and for every holomorphic value $l$ at $t/2$ of $z\mapsto L^S(B\otimes\chi_{V'}^{-1},z+\frac12)$,
\[
\mathrm{pet}\bigl(\theta_{\Phi_1}(u_1),\theta_{\Phi_2}(u_2)\bigr)=\vol(V')\,\frac{l}{d^S_{\rank V,\rank V'}(t/2)}\int_{\mathcal O_S}
\langle\omega(g)\Phi_1,\Phi_2\rangle\,\langle u_1,u_2\rangle(g)\,dm(g),
\]
$\vol(V')$ denoting the total mass of the automorphic quotient of $\Uni(V')$ for $m'$.
\end{definition}

This is the Rallis inner product formula in the form given by the doubling method \cite{Rallis1987,PiatetskiShapiroRallis1987,KudlaRallis1994}; it
is derived from the theta package $\mathcal H_\Theta$ in \lref{GlobalTheory.Core.Rk.IP.heckeForm_of_doubling'} (from the doubling
package, the Siegel--Weil formula and the unramified doubling computation), and it is a hypothesis of the theorem below.

\begin{theorem}[Inner products along twisted theta chains]\label{pf:bundles:thm:chain}
Assume Harish-Chandra finiteness, rapid decay of cusp forms, the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, Howe's
unramified statement and the unramified doubling computation for $\GL_n$. Let $D$ be a datum with real data $R$ and face satisfying the
standing hypotheses of the development (core conditions, a parameter choice, absolutely irreducible characters, good types, the guard,
and gaps of at least $100r^4$ between consecutive Harish-Chandra parameters), the node gap condition (there is $N\ge\rank X'+1$ such that
consecutive entries $b_i(\tau)<b_{i+1}(\tau)$ of the Harish-Chandra parameters of the chain differ by at least $t+2N^3+1$) and the Rallis
inner product formula (\cref{pf:bundles:def:rallis}). Then $D$ has chain scalars.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.thetaChain_steps_positive_multiple_of_LValue}
\end{theorem}

\subsubsection{Step statements}

The proof of \cref{pf:bundles:thm:chain} reduces it to a list of statements about the steps of chain data. In the following
definition, a \emph{step configuration} consists of an admissible chain datum $\bundc$ with a twist $\bundw$, an integer $k<m$, stages
$s,s'$ at steps $k$ and $k+1$ and a step $p$ from $s$ to $s'$ of index $k+1$; metrics $(\,,)_s$, $(\,,)_D$ are linear in the first and
conjugate-linear in the second variable. Given metrics on $s$ and $s'$, an element $x\in C(s')$ is an \emph{adjoint lift} of $u\in C(s)$ if
$(y,x)_{s'}=(\iota_p y,u)_s$ for all $y\in C(s')$; for a polynomial $\phi$ and a vector $v$ of $s$, a vector $w_0$ of $s'$ is \emph{adjoint
to $v$} if $w_0$ is $K$-finite and $(w,w_0)_D=(I_\phi w,v)_D$ for all $K$-finite $w$. We use the local quantities at a place
$\mathfrak P\in Q$, with $N=\Nm\mathfrak P$ and $z_i=(\mu_i\mu_{k+1}^{-1})(\mathfrak P)$:
\begin{gather*}
\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)=\prod_{i=1}^k\prod_{n=1}^t\bigl((1-z_iN^{-n})(1-z_i^{-1}N^{-n})\bigr)^{-1},\qquad
\mathrm{loc}_k=\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t),\\
\mathrm{idx}_{\mathfrak P}(j)=\frac{N^{rjt}\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,r+jt)}{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,r)\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,jt)},\qquad
\mathrm{v}_{\mathfrak P}(j)=\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,r)\,N^{-rjt}.
\end{gather*}
A number $H$ is an \emph{$H$-value} of $(\bundc,S,k)$ if $H=\prod_{i=1}^k\prod_{n=1}^t\Lambda_i(n)$ for entire functions $\Lambda_i$ with
$\Lambda_i(s)=L^S(s,\mu_i\mu_{k+1}^{-1})$ (the Euler product over the places of $E$ above places outside $S$) for $\Re s>1$. For a
Haar measure $m$ of covolume one on the space of the Weil representation, a Haar measure $m_\R$ on the real group adapted to the level
(integrals of level-invariant functions over the level set equal integrals over the real group), a polynomial $\phi$ and a pure tensor $v$
of $K$-finite vectors, let
\[
I(p,\phi,v)=\int\langle\omega(g)\Phi_\phi,\Phi_\phi\rangle\,\overline{\textstyle\prod_\tau\langle v_\tau,\pi_\tau(g_\tau)v_\tau\rangle}\,dm_\R(g)
\]
(\emph{real zeta integral}), where real convergence of $(\phi,v)$ means that the integrand times a power $\mathrm{ht}^{s_1-t/2}$ of the
height is integrable for some $s_1<t/2$ and every majorant, and put
\[
\kappa(p,\phi,v,H_0)=\frac{\vol(s')}{\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\mathrm{idx}_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)}\cdot\frac{H_0}{d^{S_0}(t/2)}\cdot
\frac{I(p,\phi,v)}{\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\mathrm v_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)},
\]
with $d^{S}=d^S_{\rank V_s,\rank V_{s'}}$.

\begin{definition}[Step statements]\label{pf:bundles:def:steps}
The following are statements about all step configurations (for (S1) and (S7), about all stages at steps $j\le m$).
\begin{enumerate}[label=(S\arabic*)]
\item \emph{Soft properties}: $C(s)$ is finite-dimensional and carries a metric; if $x(w)=0$ for some $w\ne0$ then $x=0$; if a non-zero
component $x(w)$ has parameters $B$, then $x$ has parameters $B$.
\item \emph{Span}: upward lifts $\theta_\phi(u(v))$ of components at $K$-finite vectors lie in the span of the components of $C(s')$ at
$K$-finite vectors.
\item \emph{Adjointness}: $\mathrm{pet}_{s'}(y(w),\theta_\phi(u(v)))=\mathrm{pet}_s(\iota_p(y)(I_\phi w),u(v))$ for $K$-finite $v,w$.
\item \emph{Value of the lift}: for metrics on $s,s'$ and $K$-finite $v$: if $\theta_\phi(u(v))\ne0$ for some $u$, there is $w_0\ne0$ adjoint to
$v$; and for every $w_0$ adjoint to $v$ and every adjoint lift $x$ of $u$, $\theta_\phi(u(v))=x(w_0)$.
\item \emph{Parameters of lifts}: if $u$ has parameters $B$ and $\theta_\phi(u(v))\ne0$, then $\theta_\phi(u(v))$ has parameters
$\mathrm{st}_{k+1}(B)$, where $\mathrm{st}_{j}(B)_w=B_w\chi^S_{j}(w)\chi^T_{j}(w)^{-1}+\mathrm{seg}_w(\chi^S_{j}(w),t)$.
\item \emph{Twisted parameters of lifts}: if $u$ has the twisted local parameters of step $k$, then so have the components
$\theta_\phi(u(v))$ for step $k+1$.
\item \emph{Twisted parameters from one value}: if a non-zero component of $x\in C(s)$ has the twisted local parameters of step $j$, so has
$x$; for $j=0$ every $x$ has them.
\item \emph{Peeling the twisted places}: for $u_1$ with the twisted parameters of step $k$ and real $z>t/2-1$, integrability of the doubling
integrand $\mathcal I_z$ of $(u_1(v_1),u_2(v_2))$ on the level set implies its integrability on the set $\mathcal O_S$ of elements integral outside
$S_\bundc$, and for $z=t/2$, $\int_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal I_{t/2}=\mathrm{loc}_k\int_{\mathrm{lev}}\mathcal I_{t/2}$.
\item \emph{Level integral}: for real convergent $(\phi,v)$ there is $s_1<t/2$ such that the doubling integrands of $(u_1(v),u_2(v))$ are
integrable on the level set for $z\ge s_1$, and $\int_{\mathrm{lev}}\mathcal I_{t/2}=I(p,\phi,v)\,\overline{(u_1,u_2)_s}$.
\item \emph{Real zeta integrals}: for every $m$ of covolume one there are $\phi,v$ such that $I(p,\phi,v)\ne0$ and $(\phi,v)$ is real convergent,
for every Haar measure $m_\R$.
\item \emph{Factorisation of $L$-values} (\cref{pf:bundles:lem:lfull} below).
\item \emph{Restoring Euler factors}: if $H$ and $H_0$ are $H$-values of $(\bundc,S_\bundc,k)$ and $(\bundc,S_0,k)$, then
$H_0=H\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)$; some $H$-value $H_0$ of $(\bundc,S_0,k)$ is non-zero; $d^{S_0}(t/2)\ne0$; and
$d^{S_\bundc}(t/2)=d^{S_0}(t/2)\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,r+(k+1)t)$.
\item \emph{Positivity at the base}: if $u\in C(s)$, $s$ at step $0$, has parameters $B$, then $B$ is bounded with exponent $r/2$ outside
$S_\bundc$, and for every $k<m$ the value $L^{S_\bundc}(B\otimes\mu_{k+1}^{-1},(1+t)/2)$ is a positive real number.
\end{enumerate}
For the real data $R$ of the datum we use further statements about all tuples $\bundt$; $\bundt_0$ denotes the base tuple of $R$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(R\arabic*)]
\item the top stage is the image of $H_X(\bundt)$, and the stages and steps of $R$ form an admissible chain with its twist;
\item there is $\delta>0$ such that $H_X(\bundt)\to C(s_{\bundt,m})$ is bijective and $\langle x,y\rangle=\delta\,(y,x)$ for every metric of
the top stage;
\item for every $u$ in the row part $E_\lambda(\bundt)$, $u\in C(s_{\bundt,0})$, and $u$ has parameters $B$ for every family $B$ of Satake
parameters of $\lambda$ if $u\ne0$; and $E_\lambda(\bundt)\neq0$ implies that $\lambda$ has such a family;
\item for every metric of the stage $s_{\bundt,k}$, the Petersson product of \cref{pf:bundles:def:scalars} is $\langle u,u'\rangle_k=(u',u)$;
\item for every $j<m$ there is a Haar measure on the real group adapted to the level of $s_{\bundt,j}$ for every $\bundt$;
\item $\vol(s_{\bundt,j})=\vol(s_{\bundt_0,j})\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q_\bundt}\mathrm{idx}_{\mathfrak P}(j)>0$ for $j<m$, and the same for the top
stage with $j=m$;
\item for every $m$ of covolume one, the norm $\|\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}\|^2$ of the step vector of $p_{\bundt,k}$ equals
$\|\mathrm v_{\bundt_0,k}\|^2\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q_\bundt}\mathrm v_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)$, and the same for the top step.
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Uniformity in the tuple} (U) is the statement: for every step $k$ there are $K\ne0$, $\phi$ and $v$ such that for every $\bundt$ there are
$m,m_\R$ and an $H$-value $H_0$ of $(\bundc_\bundt,S_0,k)$ with $(\phi,v)$ real convergent for $p_{\bundt,k}$ and $\kappa(p_{\bundt,k},\phi,v,H_0)=K$;
and the same for the top step.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Adjointness of the lifts]\label{pf:bundles:lem:adj}
Rapid decay of cusp forms implies (S3).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.chainThetaLiftsAdjoint_of_cuspRapidDecay}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $u\in C(s)$, $y\in C(s')$ and $K$-finite $v,w$. By definition of the upward lift,
\[
\mathrm{pet}_{s'}\bigl(y(w),\theta_\phi(u(v))\bigr)=\int_{\mathcal F_{s'}}\int_{\mathcal F_s}\theta(g,h;\Phi_\phi)\,y(w)(h)\,
\overline{u(v)(g)}\,d\nu_s(g)\,d\nu_{s'}(h).
\]
The components $u(v)$ and $y(w)$ are of rapid decay (they are components of automorphic maps of the stages, and the first hypothesis
applies), so the two integrations may be interchanged (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.thetaKernel_iterated_integrals_swap}, which proves
the interchange for the theta kernel of a Weil representation and functions of rapid decay on the two fundamental domains). After the
interchange the inner integral is $\int_{\mathcal F_{s'}}\theta(g,h;\Phi_\phi)y(w)(h)\,d\nu_{s'}(h)=\iota_p(y)(I_\phi w)(g)$ by the defining
property of the downward lift, and the outer integral is $\mathrm{pet}_s(\iota_p(y)(I_\phi w),u(v))$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Value of the lift]\label{pf:bundles:lem:upval}
(S1), (S2) and (S3) imply (S4).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.chain_theta_lift_up_eq_adjoint_value}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix a step configuration with metrics on $s$ and $s'$, a polynomial $\phi$ and a $K$-finite $v$; put $l(w)=(I_\phi w,v)_D$. Let $u\in C(s)$,
let $x$ be an adjoint lift of $u$, and put $F=\theta_\phi(u(v))$. For $y\in C(s')$ and $K$-finite $w$, (S3) and the Schur relation give
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:upval}
\mathrm{pet}_{s'}(y(w),F)=\mathrm{pet}_s(\iota_p(y)(I_\phi w),u(v))=(\iota_py,u)_s\,l(w)=(y,x)_{s'}\,l(w).
\end{equation}
By (S2), $F$ lies in the span of the components $y(w)$ ($y\in C(s')$, $w$ $K$-finite). The following linear-algebra fact applies
(\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.value_of_adjoint_of_mem_span}, proved by expanding $F$ in an orthonormal basis of $C(s')$ and using the
Schur relation): if $F\neq0$ lies in this span and satisfies \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:upval} for all $y,w$, then $x\ne0$ and $F=x(w_1)$ for
some $K$-finite $w_1$. Such a $w_1$ is adjoint to $v$: for $K$-finite $w$, \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:upval} with $y=x$ and the Schur relation
give $(x,x)_{s'}(w,w_1)_D=\mathrm{pet}_{s'}(x(w),x(w_1))=(x,x)_{s'}l(w)$, and $(x,x)_{s'}\ne0$.

First assertion of (S4): if $F\ne0$ for some $u$, take for $x$ the adjoint lift of $u$, which exists because the metrics are positive definite
and $C(s')$ is finite-dimensional (\lref{GlobalTheory.hermitian_adjoint_exists_unique}); the vector $w_1$ obtained above is adjoint to $v$ and
non-zero, as $F=x(w_1)\ne0$.

Second assertion: let $w_0$ be adjoint to $v$ and $x$ an adjoint lift of $u$. If $F=0$, then \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:upval} with $y=x$, $w=w_0$ gives
$0=(x,x)_{s'}\,l(w_0)=(x,x)_{s'}(w_0,w_0)_D$, so $x=0$ or $w_0=0$, and in both cases $x(w_0)=0=F$. If $F\neq0$, then $F=x(w_1)$ with $w_1$ adjoint to
$v$; adjoint vectors are unique (if $w_0,w_1$ are adjoint to $v$, then $(w_1-w_0,w_1-w_0)_D=0$), so $w_1=w_0$ and $F=x(w_0)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Factorisation of $L$-values]\label{pf:bundles:lem:lfull}
Let $\bundc$ be an admissible chain datum, $S=S_\bundc$, $B$ a parameter family bounded with exponent $r/2$ outside $S$, and $k<m$. There is
a non-zero $H$-value $H$ of $(\bundc,S,k)$ such that $L^S(B\otimes\mu_{k+1}^{-1},s)$ is a convergent Euler product at $s=(1+t)/2$ with
non-zero value $L$, and the function $z\mapsto L^S(\mathrm{ch}_k(B)\otimes(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1},z+\frac12)$ has holomorphic value $L\cdot H$ at
$z=t/2$.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.twistedThetaChain_LValue_quotientFactorization}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For $1\le i\le k$ the partial $L$-function $L^S(s,\mu_i\mu_{k+1}^{-1})$ extends to an entire function $\Lambda_i$ with $\Lambda_i(n)\ne0$ for
$1\le n\le t$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.thetaChain_char_quotient_LFunction_entire_ne_zero}, which proves this for the non-trivial
characters $\mu_i\mu_j^{-1}$, $1\le i<j\le m$). Put $A=B\otimes\mu_{k+1}^{-1}$; it is bounded with exponent $r/2$ outside $S$, because the
values of a unitary character have absolute value $1$. The parameter family of step $k$ twisted by $(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1}$ decomposes as
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:chtwist}
\mathrm{ch}_k(B)\otimes(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1}=A+\sum_{i=1}^k\mathrm{seg}\bigl(\mu_i\mu_{k+1}^{-1},t\bigr)
\end{equation}
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.thetaLiftChainHeckeParameter_twist_next_target_char}, a computation with the recursion defining
$\mathrm{ch}_k$ and the splitting characters). Euler products of sums of families are products of Euler products where they converge, and
the Euler product of a segment family at $s$ is $\prod_{l=0}^{t-1}L^S(s+\frac{t-1}2-l,\cdot)$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.eulerProd_add_segFam}). Hence the function
\[
G(x)=L^S(A,x+\tfrac12)\prod_{i=1}^k\prod_{l=0}^{t-1}\Lambda_i\bigl(x+\tfrac12+\tfrac{t-1}2-l\bigr)
\]
agrees with $L^S(\mathrm{ch}_k(B)\otimes(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1},x+\frac12)$ for $\Re x>r+t+2$: there $\Re(x+\frac12+\frac{t-1}2-l)>1$ for $l<t$,
so all Euler products converge. Since $A$ is bounded with exponent $r/2$, $L^S(A,s)$ is holomorphic on $\Re s>\frac r2+1$; hence $G$ is
holomorphic on $\Re x>\frac{r+1}2$, a right half-plane containing $t/2$ (as $t\ge r+5$). At $x=t/2$ the reindexing $n=t-l$ gives
$G(t/2)=L^S(A,\frac{1+t}2)\,H$ with $H=\prod_{i=1}^k\prod_{n=1}^t\Lambda_i(n)$, an $H$-value of $(\bundc,S,k)$, non-zero by the choice of the
$\Lambda_i$. Finally $\Re\frac{1+t}2>\frac r2+1$, so $L^S(A,s)$ is a convergent Euler product at $\frac{1+t}2$, and a convergent Euler product
with non-vanishing factors is non-zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The inner product at one step]\label{pf:bundles:lem:exact}
Assume the Rallis inner product formula for $D$, (S1), (S8) and (S9). Then for every step configuration, metric on $s$, $m$ of covolume one,
adapted $m_\R$, real convergent $(\phi,v)$, $u_1,u_2\in C(s)$ and family $B$ such that $u_1$ has parameters $B$ and the twisted parameters of
step $k$, if there is a split place $q\notin S_\bundc$ at which $B$ avoids the values excluded in \cref{pf:bundles:def:rallis} for
$\chi^T_{k+1}$, and $l$ is a holomorphic value at $t/2$ of $z\mapsto L^{S_\bundc}(B\otimes(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1},z+\frac12)$, then
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:exact}
\mathrm{pet}_{s'}\bigl(\theta_\phi(u_1(v)),\theta_\phi(u_2(v))\bigr)=\overline{\vol(s')\,\frac{l}{d^{S_\bundc}(t/2)}\,\mathrm{loc}_k\,
I(p,\phi,v)}\;(u_1,u_2)_s .
\end{equation}
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.thetaLiftChainStep_exact_innerProduct}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If the pure tensor $v$ is $0$, some $v_\tau$ is $0$; then the integrand of $I(p,\phi,v)$ vanishes and both sides of \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:exact}
are $0$. Otherwise $u_1(v)\ne0$ by (S1), since $u_1\ne0$, so $u_1(v)$ has parameters $B$. We apply the Rallis inner product formula to the pair
$(V_s,V_{s'})$ (of ranks $r+kt$ and $r+(k+1)t$, $t\ge2$), the Weil representation of the step, the test vectors $\Phi_1=\Phi_2=\Phi_\phi$ and the
functions $\overline{u_1(v)},\overline{u_2(v)}$. Its hypotheses hold: the forms of a stage of the twisted level are forms of the finer level
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.chain_stage_forms_twisted_level_le_finer_level}), and the conjugates of their components are cusp forms with respect
to a Cartan frame, with a majorant (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.conj_stage_val_mem_A0_and_cartan_frame}); the Witt index of $V_{s'}$ is less
than $t$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.WittTwo.wittIndex_lt_and_two_split}); the test vectors are Fock vectors, spherical outside
$S_\bundc$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.twistedChainStep_fockVectors_spherical_offS}); $\overline{u_2(v)}$ is spherical outside $S_\bundc$ by the
level conditions of the stage; and the doubling integrand is integrable on $\mathcal O_S$ at some $z<t/2$ by (S9) and (S8) (take
$z=\max(s_1,\frac t2-\frac12)$). The formula gives
\[
\mathrm{pet}\bigl(\theta_\phi(\overline{u_1(v)}),\theta_\phi(\overline{u_2(v)})\bigr)=\vol(s')\frac{l}{d^{S_\bundc}(t/2)}\int_{\mathcal O_S}
\mathcal I_{t/2},
\]
since at $z=t/2$ the doubling integrand is the product of the Weil coefficient and the matrix coefficient. By (S8) and (S9),
$\int_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal I_{t/2}=\mathrm{loc}_k\,I(p,\phi,v)\,\overline{(u_1,u_2)_s}$. The upward lift of the conjugate of a left-invariant
function is the conjugate of its upward lift, and $\mathrm{pet}(\bar f_1,\bar f_2)=\overline{\mathrm{pet}(f_1,f_2)}$ for left-invariant
functions (both by the invariance of the theta kernel and of the fundamental domain). Taking complex conjugates gives
\eqref{pf:bundles:eq:exact}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The step scalar]\label{pf:bundles:lem:steplam}
Assume (S1), (S4)--(S7), (S11), (S12) and the conclusion of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:exact}. Let a step configuration with metrics on $s$ and $s'$,
$m$, $m_\R$, real convergent $(\phi,v)$ and an $H$-value $H_0$ of $(\bundc,S_0,k)$ with $\kappa=\kappa(p,\phi,v,H_0)\ne0$ be given, let $B$ be
bounded with exponent $r/2$ outside $S_\bundc$, and let $u_1\in C(s)$ have parameters $\mathrm{ch}_k(B)$ and the twisted parameters of step $k$.
Put $L=L^{S_\bundc}(B\otimes\mu_{k+1}^{-1},\frac{1+t}2)$. There is $w_0\ne0$ adjoint to $v$ such that every adjoint lift $x_1$ of $u_1$ has
parameters $\mathrm{ch}_{k+1}(B)$ and the twisted parameters of step $k+1$, and for all $u_2\in C(s)$ with adjoint lift $x_2$,
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:steplam}
(x_1,x_2)_{s'}\,(w_0,w_0)_D=\overline{\kappa\,L}\,(u_1,u_2)_s .
\end{equation}
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.twistedThetaChain_step_adjoint_scalar}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write $S=S_\bundc$. By \cref{pf:bundles:lem:lfull} there is an $H$-value $H$ of $(\bundc,S,k)$ with $L\ne0$ such that
$z\mapsto L^S(\mathrm{ch}_k(B)\otimes(\chi^T_{k+1})^{-1},z+\frac12)$ has holomorphic value $LH$ at $t/2$. The family inverse to $B$ is bounded
(\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainTw.invFam_bounded}), and therefore there is a split place $q\notin S$ at which $\mathrm{ch}_k(B)$ avoids the excluded
values for $\chi^T_{k+1}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.chain_each_step_splitPlace_without_excludedParams}). \Cref{pf:bundles:lem:exact}, applied to
$\mathrm{ch}_k(B)$ and $l=LH$, gives for every $u_2$
\[
\mathrm{pet}_{s'}\bigl(\theta_\phi(u_1(v)),\theta_\phi(u_2(v))\bigr)=\overline{\vol(s')\,\frac{LH}{d^S(t/2)}\,\mathrm{loc}_k\,I}\,(u_1,u_2)_s,
\qquad I=I(p,\phi,v).
\]
The constant equals $\kappa L$. Indeed, by (S12), $H_0=H\prod_{\mathfrak P}\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)$ and $d^S=d^{S_0}\prod_{\mathfrak
P}\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,r+(k+1)t)$, and from the definitions
\[
\mathrm{idx}_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)\,\mathrm v_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)=\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,r+(k+1)t)}{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,(k+1)t)}=
\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,r+(k+1)t)}{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t)},
\]
so that $\vol(s')\frac{LH}{d^S}\mathrm{loc}_kI=\vol(s')\frac{LH_0}{d^{S_0}}\prod_{\mathfrak P}\frac{\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t)}{\zeta_{\mathfrak
P}(t,r+(k+1)t)}I=\kappa L$. Thus
\begin{equation}\label{pf:bundles:eq:ex}
\mathrm{pet}_{s'}\bigl(\theta_\phi(u_1(v)),\theta_\phi(u_2(v))\bigr)=\overline{\kappa L}\,(u_1,u_2)_s\qquad(u_2\in C(s)).
\end{equation}
For $u_2=u_1$ the right-hand side is non-zero, as $\kappa L\ne0$ and $(u_1,u_1)_s>0$; hence $\theta_\phi(u_1(v))\ne0$. By (S4) there is
$w_0\ne0$ adjoint to $v$, and for every adjoint lift $x$ of an element $u$ we have $\theta_\phi(u(v))=x(w_0)$. Let $x_1$ be an adjoint lift of $u_1$.
Then $x_1(w_0)=\theta_\phi(u_1(v))$ is non-zero and has parameters $\mathrm{ch}_{k+1}(B)$ by (S5) and the twisted parameters of step $k+1$ by (S6);
by (S1) and (S7), $x_1$ has the same properties. For $u_2$ with adjoint lift $x_2$, \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:ex} and the Schur relation of the
metric give $(x_1,x_2)_{s'}(w_0,w_0)_D=\mathrm{pet}_{s'}(x_1(w_0),x_2(w_0))=\overline{\kappa L}(u_1,u_2)_s$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Uniformity in the tuple]\label{pf:bundles:lem:unif}
Assume the standing hypotheses, the node gap condition, (S10), (S12), (R1) and (R5)--(R7). Then (U) holds.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.twistedChain_step_constants_tuple_indep}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We treat a step $k$ with $k+1<m$; the top step is identical. Choose $m$ of covolume one (it exists). By (S10) for the base tuple $\bundt_0$ there
are $\phi,v$ with $I(p_{\bundt_0,k},\phi,v)\ne0$ and $(\phi,v)$ real convergent for every Haar measure; let $m_\R$ be the measure of (R5), adapted
to the level for all tuples. By (S12) for $\bundt_0$ there is a non-zero $H$-value $H_0$ of $(\bundc_{\bundt_0},S_0,k)$, and $d^{S_0}(t/2)\ne0$.
Since the $H$-values only involve the ratios $\mu_i\mu_{k+1}^{-1}$, which do not depend on the tuple, $H_0$ is an $H$-value of
$(\bundc_\bundt,S_0,k)$ for every $\bundt$; likewise $S_0$ and $d^{S_0}$ do not depend on $\bundt$.

For every tuple the Weil representation of $p_{\bundt,k}$ has the same exponents and its step vector $\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}$ is a vacuum vector
(\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainTw.StepExpTw.twStepExpVac}). For two such Weil representations the Weil coefficients of the polynomial test
vectors are proportional, with the squares of the norms of the step vectors as factors:
\[
\langle\omega_\bundt(g)\Phi^\bundt_\phi,\Phi^\bundt_\phi\rangle\,\|\mathrm v_{\bundt_0,k}\|^2=\langle\omega_{\bundt_0}(g)\Phi^{\bundt_0}_\phi,
\Phi^{\bundt_0}_\phi\rangle\,\|\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}\|^2
\]
(\lref{GlobalTheory.FockCoefIndep.coef_indep_exponents}, which proves this identity for Weil representations with the same exponents
and vacuum step vectors); the norms are non-zero (\lref{GlobalTheory.FockProdCoef.ipTh_ptOf_glue}, which computes them as Gaussian
integrals). Consequently the integrands of $I(p_{\bundt,k},\phi,v)$ and $I(p_{\bundt_0,k},\phi,v)$ are proportional, $(\phi,v)$ is real
convergent for every $\bundt$, and by (R7)
\[
I(p_{\bundt,k},\phi,v)=I(p_{\bundt_0,k},\phi,v)\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q_\bundt}\mathrm v_{\mathfrak P}(k+1),
\]
where the product is non-zero because the norm of $\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}$ is. By (R6), $\vol(s_{\bundt,k+1})=\vol(s_{\bundt_0,k+1})\prod_{\mathfrak
P}\mathrm{idx}_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)$ with non-zero product. Substituting into the definition of $\kappa$,
\[
\kappa(p_{\bundt,k},\phi,v,H_0)=\vol(s_{\bundt_0,k+1})\,\frac{H_0}{d^{S_0}(t/2)}\,I(p_{\bundt_0,k},\phi,v)=:K,
\]
which does not depend on $\bundt$, and $K\ne0$ because $\vol(s_{\bundt_0,k+1})>0$, $H_0\ne0$, $d^{S_0}(t/2)\neq0$ and
$I(p_{\bundt_0,k},\phi,v)\ne0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Assembly of the chain scalars]\label{pf:bundles:prop:assembly}
Let $D$, $R$ satisfy the standing hypotheses and the node gap condition, and assume (S1), (S7), (S11), (S13), the conclusion of
\cref{pf:bundles:lem:steplam} for all step configurations, (R1)--(R4) and (U). Then $D$ has chain scalars.
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.chainStep_scalars_of_realisation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Fix a tuple $\bundt$, a system $\lambda$ and a family $B$ of Satake parameters of $\lambda$ outside $S_\bundt$, bounded with exponent $r/2$. By
(R4), $x$ is a lift of $u$ at step $k$ if and only if $x$ is an adjoint lift of $u$ for the metrics of the stages; the metrics are positive
definite and the spaces finite-dimensional (S1), so every $u$ has a unique adjoint lift, namely the value at $u$ of the adjoint of
$\iota_{p_{\bundt,k}}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.hermitian_adjoint_exists_unique}, existence and uniqueness of adjoints for positive definite
hermitian forms).

\emph{Claim.} For every $k<m$: every $u\in\mathcal R_k$ lies in $C(s_{\bundt,k})$ and is either $0$ or has parameters $\mathrm{ch}_k(B)$ and the
twisted parameters of step $k$; and if $E_\lambda(\bundt)\ne0$ then $\mathcal R_k$ contains a non-zero element. For $k=0$ this is (R3) and (S7).
Let $x\in\mathcal R_{k+1}$ be a lift of $u\in\mathcal R_k$. If $u=0$ then $(y,x)=(\iota y,0)=0$ for all $y$, so $x=0$. Otherwise the
conclusion of \cref{pf:bundles:lem:steplam}, applied with the data $K\neq0,\phi,v$ and $m,m_\R,H_0$ of (U), shows that $x$ has parameters
$\mathrm{ch}_{k+1}(B)$ and the twisted parameters of step $k+1$. If $u\in\mathcal R_k$ is non-zero, its adjoint lift lies in $\mathcal R_{k+1}$
and has parameters, so it is non-zero.

\emph{Middle steps.} Let $k+1<m$ and let $K,\phi,v$ be given by (U). Let $(\,,)_D$, $(\,,)'_D$ be the forms on the vectors of the stages $k$,
$k+1$; they are determined by their values on pure tensors and do not depend on $\bundt$. Assume $E_\lambda(\bundt)\ne0$. Choose $0\ne u_0\in
E_\lambda(\bundt)$; by (R3) it has parameters $B$, so by (S13) $B$ is bounded with exponent $r/2$ and $l=L^{S_\bundt}(B\otimes\mu_{\bundt,k+1}^{-1},
\frac{1+t}2)$ is positive, and by \cref{pf:bundles:lem:lfull} it is a convergent Euler product: $l$ is an $L$-value of $(\bundt,B,k+1)$. By the
claim there is $0\ne u_1\in\mathcal R_k$, and \cref{pf:bundles:lem:steplam} gives $w_0\ne0$ adjoint to $v$. An adjoint vector is unique (if $w_0,w_1$
are adjoint to $v$, then $(w_0-w_1,w_0-w_1)'_D=0$), and the defining property of adjoint vectors involves only $(\,,)_D$, $(\,,)'_D$, $v$ and the
intertwiner $I_\phi$, none of which depends on $\bundt$, $\lambda$ or $B$. Hence $w_0$ is the same for all $(\bundt,\lambda,B)$. For the adjoint lift
$x_1$ of $u_1$, \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:steplam} gives $(x_1,x_1)'(w_0,w_0)'_D=\overline{Kl}(u_1,u_1)$ with all three forms positive on non-zero
vectors and $l>0$; therefore $q_k=K/(w_0,w_0)'_D$ is a positive real number. If $(\bundt,\lambda,B)$ as above do not exist, put $q_k=1$.

Now let $(\bundt,\lambda,B)$ be arbitrary with $E_\lambda(\bundt)\ne0$, and $l$ as above. Every $u\in\mathcal R_k$ has a lift. Let $u_1,u_2\in\mathcal
R_k$ with lifts $x_1,x_2$. If $u_1=0$ then $x_1=0$ and the identity below is trivial. Otherwise \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:steplam}, with the same
$w_0$, gives $(x_1,x_2)'=\overline{q_kl}\,(u_1,u_2)=q_kl\,(u_1,u_2)$; by (R4) and hermitian symmetry this is $\langle x_1,x_2\rangle_{k+1}=q_kl\,\langle
u_1,u_2\rangle_k$. So the step statement holds with $\kappa_{k+1}=q_k$.

\emph{Top step.} The same argument for the top step, with the top stage, (R2) and top lifts: by (R2), $x$ is a top lift of $u$ if and only if
$\delta$ times its image in the top stage is the adjoint lift of $u$. It gives $q>0$ such that $\langle x_1,x_2\rangle=(q/\delta)\,l\,\langle
u_1,u_2\rangle_{m-1}$ for top lifts, with $l$ the $L$-value of $(\bundt,B,m)$; put $\kappa_m=q/\delta$.

The numbers $\kappa_1,\dots,\kappa_m$ are positive and independent of $(\bundt,\lambda,B)$; together with the existence of Satake families when
$E_\lambda(\bundt)\ne0$ (R3), this is the statement that $D$ has chain scalars.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{The twisted places}\label{pf:bundles:twisted}

We prove the statements (S6), (S8) and (R7), which concern the twisted places. Let $\mathfrak P\in Q$ lie over the place $v$ of $F$. Then $v$
splits in $E$, and a \emph{split projection} at $\mathfrak P$ identifies $E_v$ with $F_v\times F_v$, the first factor corresponding to
$\mathfrak P$, and hence $\Uni(V_s)(F_v)$ with $\GL_n(F_v)$, $n=\rank V_s$. For a stage $s$ at step $k$, a \emph{chart} is a homomorphism
$\iota\colon\GL_{A\sqcup D_k}(F_v)\to\Uni(V_s)(F_v)$ compatible with a split projection, where $|A|=r$ and $D_k$ is the set of $k$ blocks of
size $t$, so that $|D_k|=kt$. Let $\varpi$ be a uniformiser of $F_v$, $q_v=\Nm\mathfrak P$ the residue cardinality, and $\xi^\flat$ a tame
character of $F_v^\times$ lifting $\xi_{\mathfrak P}$.

For a representation $\pi$ of $\GL_{A\sqcup D}(F_v)$ and a multiset $B$ of $|D|$ non-zero complex numbers, a vector $x$ is a
\emph{generalised eigenvector of type $\xi^\flat$ with parameters $B$} if $\pi(g)x=\xi^\flat(\det g_{DD})\,x$ for all $g$ in the parahoric
subgroup $\mathcal P_{A,D}$ of the decomposition $A\sqcup D$ ($g_{DD}$ denoting the $D\times D$ block of $g$), and for every subset
$\sigma\subseteq D$ the $\xi^\flat$-twisted Hecke operator of the double coset $\mathcal P_{A,D}\,\varpi^{1_\sigma}\mathcal P_{A,D}$ maps $x$ to
$q_v^{(|A||\sigma|+|\sigma|(|D|-|\sigma|))/2}\,e_{|\sigma|}(B)\,x$; here $\varpi^{1_\sigma}$ is the diagonal matrix with entries $\varpi$ on
$\sigma$ and $1$ elsewhere, and $e_j$ is the $j$-th elementary symmetric function. A function $F$ on $\Uni(V_s)(\A_F)$ has the \emph{twisted
local parameters of step $k$} if for every $\mathfrak P\in Q$, split projection, uniformiser $\varpi$, tame lift $\xi^\flat$ of
$\xi_{\mathfrak P}$ and chart $\iota$ of $s$, the conjugate $\bar F$ is a generalised eigenvector of type $\xi^\flat$ with parameters
$q_k(\mathfrak P)$ for right translation through $\iota$. In the recursion defining $q_k(\mathfrak P)$, $\chi(\varpi)$ denotes the value of
$\chi$ at the idele which is $\varpi$ at $\mathfrak P$ and $1$ elsewhere, and $\mathrm{seg}(z,t)=\{z\,q_v^{(t-1)/2-i}:0\le i<t\}$.

\begin{lemma}[Twisted parameters of lifts]\label{pf:bundles:lem:S6}
Rapid decay of cusp forms implies (S6).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.chainThetaLiftUpLowerBlockParameter_proof}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix a step configuration, $u\in C(s)$ with the twisted local parameters of step $k$, a polynomial $\phi$ and a $K$-finite vector $v_0$; put
$F=u(v_0)$ and $G=\theta_\phi(F)$. Let $\mathfrak P\in Q$ over $v$, a split projection, a uniformiser $\varpi$, a tame lift $\xi^\flat$ and a
chart $\iota'$ of $s'$ be given. We must show that $\bar G$ is a generalised eigenvector of type $\xi^\flat$ with parameters $q_{k+1}(\mathfrak P)$
for right translation through $\iota'$.

(1) \emph{Local characters.} The stage $s$ has a chart $\iota$ at $\mathfrak P$, and there are characters $\mu_S,\mu_T$ of $F_v^\times$, the local
components of $\chi^S_{k+1}$ and $\chi^T_{k+1}$ at $\mathfrak P$, with $\mu_S(\varpi)=\chi^S_{k+1}(\varpi)$ and $\mu_T(\varpi)=\chi^T_{k+1}(\varpi)$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.TwistChart.charts_at_twist}, which provides charts and local characters at the twisted places). By admissibility the
restrictions of $\chi^S_{k+1}$ and $\chi^T_{k+1}$ to the units at $\mathfrak P$ are given by the residue character
$\chi_{k+1}(\mathfrak P)^{-1}=\xi_{\mathfrak P}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.levRes_step}, the residue condition of the steps), so $\mu_S$
and $\mu_T$ lift $\xi_{\mathfrak P}$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.liftsChar_of_residueChar_at_splitPlace}). As $\xi^\flat$ lifts $\xi_{\mathfrak P}$ as
well, the characters $\mu_S(\xi^\flat)^{-1}$ and $\mu_T(\xi^\flat)^{-1}$ are unramified; and $\xi^\flat$ is tame
(\lref{GlobalTheory.tameLift_isTame_not_isUnram}).

(2) \emph{Linear model.} Let $a=r+kt$ and $b=r+(k+1)t$. There are a space $\mathcal W$ of functions on $(A\sqcup D_{k+1})\times(A\sqcup
D_k)$-matrices over $F_v$, stable under the action
$\omega_2(g,h)\varphi(x)=\mu_T(\det g)^{-1}\mu_S(\det h)\,|\det g|^{b/2}|\det h|^{-a/2}\,\varphi(h^{-1}xg)$ of
$\GL_{A\sqcup D_k}(F_v)\times\GL_{A\sqcup D_{k+1}}(F_v)$,
and a linear map $\mathcal T$ from $\mathcal W$ to the space of test functions of the Weil representation $\omega$ of the step, such that
$\omega(\iota(g),\iota'(h))\circ\mathcal T=\mathcal T\circ\omega_2(g,h)$, and such that $\mathcal W$ contains the function $\Phi^\flat$ which equals
$\xi^\flat(\det x_{AA})$ on integral matrices $x$ whose $A\times A$ block $x_{AA}$ is invertible over $\OO_v$ and whose $D_{k+1}\times A$ block has
entries in $\mathfrak p_v$, and $0$ elsewhere, with $\mathcal T(\Phi^\flat)=\Phi_\phi$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.linearModel_twistPlace_in_thetaFunctions},
the linear model of the Weil representation at a twisted place).

(3) \emph{The representation of the source.} Let $X$ be the span of the right translates of $\bar F$ through $\iota$; it is stable under right
translation through $\iota$, and we write $\rho$ for the resulting representation of $\GL_{A\sqcup D_k}(F_v)$. By hypothesis $\bar F$ is a generalised
eigenvector of type $\xi^\flat$ with parameters $q_k(\mathfrak P)$ in the space of all functions, hence also in $X$.

(4) \emph{The pairing.} For $\varphi\in\mathcal W$ and $y\in X$ put $\beta(\varphi,y)(h)=\int_{\mathcal F_s}\theta(g,h;\mathcal T\varphi)\,y(g)\,d\nu_s(g)$.
The integrals converge: $y$ is a combination of right translates of $\bar F$, and $\theta(\cdot,h;\mathcal T\varphi)$ times a right translate of $\bar F$
is integrable on $\mathcal F_s$ by rapid decay (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.ThetaFirstVar.thetaKO_mul_conj_integrableOn}); moreover every
$y\in X$ is left invariant under $\Uni(V_s)(F)$. The map $\beta$ is bilinear, and it is equivariant:
$\beta(\omega_2(g,h)\varphi,\rho(g)y)=\beta(\varphi,y)(\,\cdot\,\iota'(h))$. Indeed, by (2) and the transformation law of the theta kernel,
$\theta(g',h';\mathcal T\omega_2(g,h)\varphi)=\theta(g'\iota(g),h'\iota'(h);\mathcal T\varphi)$, and the substitution $g'\mapsto g'\iota(g)^{-1}$ is
allowed for integrals over $\mathcal F_s$ of left-invariant integrable functions (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainOnto.setIntegral_fd_mul_right}).

(5) \emph{Local transfer.} The lower-block transfer theorem (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.lowerBlockParameter_transfer_invariantPairing}: for a
non-archimedean local field with uniformiser $\varpi$, finite sets $A,D,D'$ with $|D'|=|D|+t$, a tame character $\xi$, characters $\mu_S,\mu_T$ with
$\mu_S\xi^{-1}$ and $\mu_T\xi^{-1}$ unramified, a space $\mathcal W$ as in (2) containing $\Phi^\flat$, a representation $\rho$ of
$\GL_{A\sqcup D}$ with a generalised eigenvector $x$ of type $\xi$ with parameters $B$, and an equivariant bilinear map $\beta$ into a representation
of $\GL_{A\sqcup D'}$, the vector $\beta(\Phi^\flat,x)$ is a generalised eigenvector of type $\xi$ with parameters
$B\cdot\mu_S(\varpi)\mu_T(\varpi)^{-1}+\mathrm{seg}(\mu_S(\varpi),t)$) applies with $D=D_k$, $D'=D_{k+1}$, $x=\bar F$ and $B=q_k(\mathfrak P)$, by
(1)--(4). By (1) and the recursion, the resulting parameters are
$q_k(\mathfrak P)\cdot\chi^S_{k+1}(\varpi)\chi^T_{k+1}(\varpi)^{-1}+\mathrm{seg}(\chi^S_{k+1}(\varpi),t)=q_{k+1}(\mathfrak P)$.

(6) Finally $\beta(\Phi^\flat,\bar F)(h)=\int_{\mathcal F_s}\theta(g,h;\Phi_\phi)\overline{F(g)}\,d\nu_s(g)=\overline{G(h)}$ by the definition of the upward
lift. Hence $\bar G$ is a generalised eigenvector of type $\xi^\flat$ with parameters $q_{k+1}(\mathfrak P)$ for right translation through $\iota'$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Peeling the twisted places]\label{pf:bundles:lem:S8}
The unramified doubling computation for $\GL_n$ and rapid decay of cusp forms imply (S8).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.chainTwistPlacesIntegralPeel_of_doubling}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix a step configuration, a Haar measure $m_W$ of covolume one on the space of the Weil representation, a majorant, a polynomial $\phi$, $u_1,u_2\in C(s)$ such that $u_1$ has the twisted
local parameters of step $k$, $K$-finite $v_1,v_2$ and $z>t/2-1$, and let $\mathcal I=\mathcal I_z$ be the doubling integrand of
$(u_1(v_1),u_2(v_2))$, a function on $G(\A_F)$, $G=\Uni(V_s)$. For a finite place $v$ of $F$ let $L_v\subseteq G(F_v)$ be the local level group of
$s$, and $g_v$ the component of $g$. The level set is $\mathrm{lev}=\{g\in\mathcal O_S:g_v\in L_v\text{ for all }v\in S_\bundc\}$, and
$\mathrm{lev}$ and $\mathcal O_S$ are measurable (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.thetaChainStage_finiteLevel_eq_product_localLevels}, which describes
the finite level of a stage as the product of its local levels). For $\mathfrak P\in Q$ let $v(\mathfrak P)\in S_\bundc$ be the place below;
distinct elements of $P_\bundc$ lie over distinct places of $F$.

\emph{Local factors.} For each $\mathfrak P\in Q$, with $v=v(\mathfrak P)$, there are an open subgroup $J_{\mathfrak P}$ of $G(F_v)$, a Haar
measure $\mu_{\mathfrak P}$ on $G(F_v)$ with $\mu_{\mathfrak P}(J_{\mathfrak P})=1$ and an integrable function $\lambda_{\mathfrak P}$ on $G(F_v)$
equal to $1$ on $J_{\mathfrak P}$, such that: $g_v\in L_v$ if and only if $g_v\in J_{\mathfrak P}$; $\mathcal I(xy)=\lambda_{\mathfrak P}(y)\,
\mathcal I(x)$ for $x\in\mathcal O_S$ with $x_v=1$ and $y\in G(F_v)$; and, if $z=t/2$, $\int\lambda_{\mathfrak P}\,d\mu_{\mathfrak P}=
\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t)$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.T.flatIntegrand_localFactor_splits_twistPlace}, which
deduces this from the unramified doubling computation for $\GL_n$, applied at the split place $v$ to the generalised eigenvectors of type
$\xi^\flat$ given by the twisted local parameters of $u_1$, and from rapid decay).

\emph{Induction.} For $T\subseteq Q$ let $E(T)=\{g\in\mathcal O_S:g_{v(\mathfrak P)}\in L_{v(\mathfrak P)}\text{ for all }\mathfrak P\in T\}$, a
measurable set. We show by induction on $T$: if $\mathcal I$ is integrable on $E(Q)$, then it is integrable on $E(Q\setminus T)$, and for $z=t/2$
\[
\int_{E(Q\setminus T)}\mathcal I\,d\nu_s=\Bigl(\prod_{\mathfrak P\in T}\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t)\Bigr)
\int_{E(Q)}\mathcal I\,d\nu_s .
\]
For $T=\emptyset$ there is nothing to prove. Let $T'=T\cup\{\mathfrak P\}$ with $\mathfrak P\notin T$, $v=v(\mathfrak P)$ and $E'=E(Q\setminus T')$.
The set $E'$ is invariant under right multiplication by $G(F_v)$: membership in $\mathcal O_S$ involves only places outside $S_\bundc$, while
$v\in S_\bundc$, and the remaining conditions concern the places $v(\mathfrak P')$, $\mathfrak P'\in Q\setminus T'$, which differ from $v$.
Moreover $E'\cap\{g:g_v\in J_{\mathfrak P}\}=E(Q\setminus T)$. The one-place Fubini lemma
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.integral_split_finite_place_fubini}: if $E'$ is a measurable set invariant under $G(F_v)$, $J$ an open subgroup with
$\mu(J)=1$ for a Haar measure $\mu$, $\lambda$ integrable with $\lambda=1$ on $J$, $\mathcal I(xy)=\lambda(y)\,\mathcal I(x)$ for $x\in E'$ with
$x_v=1$, and $\mathcal I$ integrable on $E'\cap\{g_v\in J\}$, then $\mathcal I$ is integrable on $E'$ and $\int_{E'}\mathcal I=\int\lambda\,d\mu
\cdot\int_{E'\cap\{g_v\in J\}}\mathcal I$) and the induction hypothesis give the assertion for $T'$.

\emph{Conclusion.} If $g\in E(Q)\setminus\mathrm{lev}$, then $g_v\notin L_v$ for some $v\in S_\bundc$; the place $v$ lies below an element of
$P_\bundc=P_g\sqcup Q$, but not below an element of $Q$ because $g\in E(Q)$; so $v$ lies below an element of $P_g$. At such places the Weil
coefficient $\langle\omega(g)\Phi_\phi,\Phi_\phi\rangle$ vanishes unless $g_v\in L_v$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.twistedChain_coefficient_supported_principal_level}, which shows that at the untwisted places the coefficient is
supported on the level group), and $\mathcal I$ contains this coefficient as a factor; hence $\mathcal I(g)=0$. Since $\mathrm{lev}\subseteq E(Q)$,
integrability on $\mathrm{lev}$ implies integrability on $E(Q)$, and the integrals over $\mathrm{lev}$ and $E(Q)$ agree. Taking $T=Q$, for which
$E(\emptyset)=\mathcal O_S$, gives (S8), as $\mathrm{loc}_k=\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\mathrm{bl}_k(\mathfrak P)\,\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(t,(k+1)t)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Norms of the step vectors]\label{pf:bundles:lem:R7}
Under the standing hypotheses, the realisation $R$ satisfies (R7).
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.realDatumNormShrink_of_scalarHypotheses}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The base tuple has no twisted places, $Q_{\bundt_0}=\emptyset$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainTw.RModel.Q_base}). Fix a tuple $\bundt$, a step $k$
with $k+1<m$ and a Haar measure $m_W$ of covolume one on the space of the Weil representation; the top step is treated in the same way, with the pair $(Y_{m-1},X')$ and $\rank X'=r+mt$. Put
$Q=Q_\bundt$. By the chain conditions of $R$, $\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}$ and $\mathrm v_{\bundt_0,k}$ are step vectors of the pair $(Y_k,Y_{k+1})$, with
its Cartan-invariant frames and the self-dual lattices given by the frames of the level conditions, for the level data of step $k+1$ with twisted
places $Q$ and $\emptyset$ respectively; $P_g$ and $Q$ are disjoint (\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainTw.RModel.disjoint_Pg_Q}), and by the guard no
element of $Q$ divides $Q_Sq\Nm(\mathfrak c_K)$.

A step vector is a pure tensor $\bigotimes_px_p$ over the places $p$ of $F$ whose component is the vacuum vector $f_0$ at the infinite places and at
the finite places not below $P_g\cup Q$, and at the place below $\mathfrak P\in P_g\cup Q$ is a local theta datum determined by the level data at
$\mathfrak P$ (\lref{GlobalTheory.StepVec.stepVecLocal}, the local description of step vectors); the places of $F$ below distinct elements
of $P_g\cup Q$ are distinct. For pure tensors the inner product is the finite product of the local inner products
(\lref{GlobalTheory.PlaceTensor.PureScalar.ip_pure_eq_finprod}), and $\langle f_0,f_0\rangle=1$ since $m_W$ has covolume one. Let $(x_p)$ be a
local decomposition of $\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}$, and put $x'_p=x_p$ for $p$ below $P_g$ and $x'_p=f_0$ otherwise. The family $(x'_p)$ satisfies the
local conditions for the twisted places $\emptyset$, so it is a local decomposition of $\mathrm v_{\bundt_0,k}$. As $x_p=x'_p$ except at the places
$v(\mathfrak P)$ below $\mathfrak P\in Q$, where $x'_p=f_0$,
\[
\|\mathrm v_{\bundt,k}\|^2=\prod_p\langle x_p,x_p\rangle=\|\mathrm v_{\bundt_0,k}\|^2\prod_{\mathfrak P\in Q}\langle x_{v(\mathfrak P)},
x_{v(\mathfrak P)}\rangle .
\]
Let $\mathfrak P\in Q$ lie over $v$. The place $v$ splits, and by the guard the ideal of the lattice of homomorphisms is a unit at $\mathfrak P$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.homLatticeIdeal_isUnit_splitPlace}). In the mixed model at the split place, $x_v$ is the function on $b\times a$ matrices
($b=\rank Y_{k+1}$, $a=\rank Y_k$) which equals $\mu(\det x_r)^{-1}$ on integral matrices whose upper left $r\times r$ block $x_r$ is invertible over
$\OO_v$ and whose lower left $(b-r)\times r$ block has entries in $\mathfrak p_v$, and $0$ elsewhere, for a unitary character $\mu$ lifting
$\xi_{\mathfrak P}^{-1}$. Its squared norm is the volume of its support, $\zeta_{\mathfrak P}(0,r)\,\Nm\mathfrak P^{-r(b-r)}$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.normSq_thetaLocalDatum_at_twistPlace}, which carries out this computation in the Weil representation). Since
$b-r=(k+1)t$, this is $\mathrm v_{\mathfrak P}(k+1)$, and (R7) follows for the step $k$.
\end{proof}

\lref{GlobalTheory.T.realTupleTwistedChain_of_scalarHypotheses}; (R2): \lref{GlobalTheory.T.realLastStageNormalized_of_scalarHypotheses};
(R3): \lref{GlobalTheory.T.rows_of_part_have_part_params_first_chain_member} (rapid decay); (R4):
\lref{GlobalTheory.T.schurProduct_typings_agree_thetaChain}; (R5)--(R7): \lref{GlobalTheory.T.realMeasureUniformInTuple_of_thetaLiftScalar},

\begin{corollary}[The adjoint scalar formula]\label{pf:bundles:cor:bform}
Assume the discrete series cover, Harish-Chandra finiteness, Harish-Chandra modules, Casselman--Wallach, rapid decay, Howe's unramified
statement, the Weil--Siegel formula, the Rallis inner product formula for all data, the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series and the unramified
doubling computation. Let $D$, $R$ satisfy the standing hypotheses, with full class group condition and $S_b=P_g$, and such that for every tuple $\bundt$ and every
place $\mathfrak P$ of $E$ with $\bar{\mathfrak P}=\mathfrak P$ which neither lies in nor is conjugate to a place of $P_g\cup Q_\bundt$, the vacuum
vectors of all the Weil representations of $R$ are fixed at $\mathfrak P$. Let $S_F$ be the set of places of $F$ below $P_g$, and
$E_\lambda\subseteq H_E$ the $\lambda$-eigenspace of the base Hilbert space outside $S_F$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item there is $\kappa_0>0$ such that for every tuple $\bundt$, every $\lambda$ and all $z_1,\dots,z_m$ such that, for each $j$, $z_j$ is the
$L$-value of $(\bundt,B_j,j)$ for some family $B_j$ of Satake parameters of $\lambda$, the composite $\Theta_\bundt\Theta_\bundt^*$ acts on
$E_\lambda$ as multiplication by $\kappa_0\prod_jz_j$;
\item $\Theta_\bundt^*$ is injective for every $\bundt$.
\end{enumerate}
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.thetaLift_adjoint_scalarFormula_injective}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The datum has chain scalars by \cref{pf:bundles:thm:chain}; the node gap condition follows from the gap condition of the parameter choice
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Core.Rk.GapOfNodes.harishChandraNodesFarApart_of_cubic_node_gap}, which deduces it from a cubic gap between the
Harish-Chandra nodes). The Weil--Siegel formula, the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series, the full class group condition and the condition
$S_b=P_g$ are not used.

(a) Put $\kappa_0=\prod_{j=1}^m\kappa_j>0$. Let $\bundt,\lambda,(z_j)$ be as in (a); we may assume $E_\lambda\ne0$, so the row part
$E_\lambda(\bundt)\supseteq E_\lambda$ is non-zero. For $0\le k<m$ let $\Lambda_k=\kappa_{k+1}z_{k+1}$; since $L$-values are unique, the chain scalars
(applied with the family $B_{k+1}$) give the step statements with the constants $\Lambda_k$ for $k+1<m$, and the top statement with $\Lambda_{m-1}$.
Iterating along the chain (the lifts of the reached vectors compute $\Theta_\bundt^*$ on the eigenspace), one obtains
$\langle\Theta_\bundt^*u_1,\Theta_\bundt^*u_2\rangle=\bigl(\prod_k\Lambda_k\bigr)\langle u_1,u_2\rangle$ for non-zero $u_1,u_2\in E_\lambda$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.ChainScalar.inner_adjoint}, which carries out this iteration using Schur orthogonality,
\lref{GlobalTheory.Hk3.schurAll_of_cuspRapidDecay}). The composite $\Theta_\bundt\Theta_\bundt^*$ preserves $E_\lambda$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.T.thetaLift_adjoint_keeps_spherical_parts_off_tuple}, which uses rapid decay, Howe's unramified statement and the fixed
vacuum vectors). For $x\in E_\lambda$ and every $y\in E_\lambda$ we get $\langle\Theta\Theta^*x-cx,y\rangle=\langle\Theta^*x,\Theta^*y\rangle-c\langle
x,y\rangle=0$ with $c=\kappa_0\prod_jz_j=\prod_k\Lambda_k$ (for $x$ or $y$ zero this is trivial), and $\Theta\Theta^*x-cx\in E_\lambda$; hence
$\Theta\Theta^*x=cx$.

(b) For each $\lambda$ with $E_\lambda(\bundt)\neq0$ the chain scalars provide $L$-values $z_1,\dots,z_m>0$, so by (a) $\Theta_\bundt\Theta_\bundt^*$
acts on $E_\lambda$ by a positive scalar $c_\lambda$; if $E_\lambda(\bundt)=0$ then $E_\lambda=0$. The eigenspaces $E_\lambda$ span $H_E$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.Hk3.OpsRows.exists_opsE}, which provides commuting Hecke operators with this spectral decomposition). Hence every
$x\in H_E$ is of the form $\Theta w$: write $x=\sum_\lambda x_\lambda$ and $w=\sum_\lambda c_\lambda^{-1}\Theta^*x_\lambda$. If $\Theta^*x=0$, then
$\langle x,x\rangle=\langle\Theta w,x\rangle=\langle w,\Theta^*x\rangle=0$, so $x=0$
(\lref{GlobalTheory.AdjointInjectiveOfParts.adjoint_inj_of_parts}, which states this linear-algebra fact).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof of the member-bundle theorem}\label{pf:bundles:proofmain}

\begin{theorem}[\cref{thm:member-bundles}]\label{pf:bundles:main}
Assume $P$ and $\sfname{RowRat}$. Let $h_L\in\sfname{RecTExists}$, $c\in\R$, and let $A$ be an at-property with $\sfname{EvLgG}(A)$. Then the
Siegel mass ratio \eqref{pf:bundles:eq:mass} holds for every CM field unramified over its maximal totally real subfield and every $M\ge3$, and,
with $\bundS_A$ the slot family of $\bundp_A=\bundp(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)\times A,\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$,
\[
\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS_A)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\sfname{ExBL}\bigl(\kappa_0,\sfname{IsBuiltE}(\bundS_A),\sfname{IsGoodE}(\bundS_A),
\sfname{BundleLinkage}(\bundp_A)\bigr).
\]
\formallabel{GlobalTheory.T.large_unramified_exponent_bundles_exist_alt}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Packages.} By \cref{pf:bundles:lem:hypA}, $P$ gives $\mathcal H_A$ (with the Siegel--Weil formula in the convergent range taken from the
second of the two Siegel--Weil components of $P$). The member package $\mathcal H_M$ consists of components of $P$. By
\cref{pf:bundles:lem:multcover}, $\mathcal H_M$, further components of $P$ and $\sfname{RowRat}$ give $\mathcal H_C$.

\emph{Mass ratio.} The first assertion is the component (A16) of $\mathcal H_A$, i.e.\ \cref{pf:bundles:prop:mass}.

\emph{Member existence.} By \cref{pf:bundles:prop:US}, $\mathcal H_A$ and $\mathcal H_C$ give the existence $(\mathrm{US})$ of unlocalised saturated
states. By \cref{pf:bundles:lem:multcond}, $\mathcal H_M$ gives the multiplicity condition for $\bundp(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c),\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$. Two
further components of $P$ (the non-endoscopic literature package and the compatibility of geometric Galois representations) give, by
\lref{GlobalTheory.T.hyps_e_third_layer_ser_n_of_geometric_galois}, the elliptic package $\mathcal H_E$ and a local Langlands package
$\mathcal H_L(h_L')$ for some $h_L'\in\sfname{RecTExists}$; this theorem states that these components imply $\mathcal H_E$, a third-layer package, a
series package, and the existence of a proof $h'_L$ of $\sfname{RecTExists}$ with $\mathcal H_L(h'_L)$. Since $\sfname{RecTExists}$ is a proposition,
$h_L'$ and $h_L$ are equal proofs, so $\mathcal H_L(h_L)$ holds. The member-existence theorem
\lref{GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.exists_baseLattice_of_prime_alt} states: $\mathcal H_A$, $\mathcal H_E$, $\mathcal H_C$, $(\mathrm{US})$, $\mathcal
H_L(h_L)$ and the multiplicity condition for $\bundp(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c),\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$ imply $\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c),
\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}}))$ (and the bundle statement for this parameter, which is not used here). Applying it with the inputs just obtained gives
$\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c),\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}}))$, and \cref{pf:bundles:lem:atprop}, with $\sfname{EvLgG}(A)$, gives
$\sfname{MemberE}(\bundS_A)$.

\emph{Bundles.} The extension $N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)$ yields $N_{\mathrm{law}}$ (an element of $N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)$ contains an element of
$N_{\mathrm{list}}(c)$, which contains one of $N_{\mathrm{law}}$), and $\bundS(N_{\mathrm{or}}(c),\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}})$ satisfies movement of positions
by \cref{pf:bundles:lem:mvor}. \Cref{pf:bundles:cor:exbl}, applied with $\mathcal H_A,\mathcal H_C,\mathcal H_M$, $N=N_{\mathrm{or}}(c)$,
$\Gamma=\Gamma_{\mathrm{cl}}$ and the at-property $A$, gives the bundle statement for $\bundp_A$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}\label{pf:bundles:rem:peculiar}
We record some features of the formal statements.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item The mass ratio in the conclusion of \cref{pf:bundles:main} is proved unconditionally (\cref{pf:bundles:prop:mass}); it is listed as a
conclusion because it is a component of the package $\mathcal H_A$. The mass of a lattice is defined to be $0$ if its genus has no finite set of
class representatives.
\item The component of $P$ named after the simple pole of Siegel Eisenstein series has the true proposition as its type; it is passed to
\cref{pf:bundles:lem:multcover,pf:bundles:lem:dm} and not used. The doubling package used here is derived from rapid decay and the continuation of
Siegel Eisenstein series (\cref{pf:bundles:lem:dm}); the component of $P$ of the same type is not used in this proof.
\item In \cref{pf:bundles:cor:bform} four hypotheses (the Weil--Siegel formula, the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series, the full class group
condition and $S_b=P_g$) are not used. The Rallis inner product formula enters \cref{pf:bundles:thm:chain} as a hypothesis; in the proof of
\cref{pf:bundles:main} it is supplied through the theta package (\cref{pf:bundles:saturated}).
\item The constant $c$ and the at-property $A$ enter only through the norm-choice extension: $c$ through the list pin and the origin data, $A$
through \cref{pf:bundles:lem:atprop,pf:bundles:lem:mvA}. The words ``large unramified exponents'' in the formal names refer to the quantifier
$\sfname{EvLgG}$ (``eventually, for large twisting data''); the exponents $k_w$ of the twisting data are not required to be large.
\item In \cref{pf:bundles:lem:exblred} the slot of the large-base condition is the trivial slot, so the bundle statement proved is the existence of
linked bundles at all good bundle points.
\end{enumerate}
\end{remark}
